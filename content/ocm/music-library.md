# Music library (phonk and more), checked 30 Oct 2026

## Which library, and why

| Library | Cost | Can we use it on our Instagram pages? | Catch |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Instagram's own library** | Free | Yes, add in the app when posting | Business accounts may see fewer tracks; can't be baked into the file |
| **Pixabay Music** (pick for now) | Free | Yes: Pixabay's licence allows social media and commercial use, no credit needed ([licence](https://pixabay.com/en/service/license/), [FAQ](https://pixabay.com/service/faq/)) | Some tracks are marked "Content ID registered"; skip those. Can't resell or redistribute the music files themselves |
| Uppbeat | Free with credit, or paid | Free plan is aimed at personal creators; ads and business use point to the Business plan ([help](https://fastly-f.uppbeat.io/help-center), [pricing](https://uppbeat.io/pricing)) | Credit line needed on the free plan; download limits unclear |
| Epidemic Sound | Paid | Personal plan covers your own channels; branded or client work needs the commercial plan ([review](https://www.creatorstackclub.com/software/epidemic-sound)) | India prices seen in third-party sources only (about ₹585 to ₹1,355 a month); check epidemicsound.com |

**Rule:** we only use music whose licence covers our own commercial pages, and we never
commit the audio files to git (the repo goes public; Pixabay doesn't allow redistributing the
files). Download at render time into `videos/**/out/`, which is not committed.
