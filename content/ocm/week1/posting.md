## Founder-led scripts (for you to record)

Phone on a stand, eye level, one take, under 30 seconds, plain room. Say it your way; these
are the points, not lines to read.

**Script A, 1 Nov: why this exists.**
1. "I'm building free apps, and I mean actually free: no ads, no selling your data."
2. One reason, from your own life: what made you start.
3. "OpenPrep for CAT, GoodHabit for money, Tomorrow for sending anything to anyone."
4. "Link's in the bio. Tell me what's broken."

**Script B, 3 Nov: how it's paid for.**
1. "People ask how free apps make money. Honest answer:"
2. The real plan, in one or two lines (the Pass and paid extras; never ads).
3. "Everything else stays free."

**Script C, 5 Nov: built in the open.**
1. "Every app's code goes public at launch."
2. Why that matters to you: anyone can check what the apps do with their data.
3. "If you build things, come build with us."
