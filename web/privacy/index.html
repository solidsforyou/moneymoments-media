Privacy

Applies to OpenSource Everything and its apps: Arena, OpenPrep, and Tomorrow and GoodHabit once they open. Draft for launch; a lawyer reviews it before we go live.

What we store

  • Your account: name, ID, age, a hashed password (we can't read it), and your app IDs.
  • If you log in with Telegram: your Telegram user ID, name and username. Never your phone number or your Telegram chats.
  • What you send and post: messages, files you send, posts and likes.

What we never do

  • No ads, and no selling or sharing your data with advertisers.
  • No reading your messages to target anything.

Who can see what

  • Messages and files: only you and the people in the chat. Files sent to "My devices" are only yours.
  • Posts: anyone on Tomorrow. Like counts are only shown to the person who posted.

Arena and children

  • Anyone can play Arena without an account. A guest's player, class and levels stay on their own device; our server sees only the answers to each round, with no name attached.
  • With an account, Arena keeps your player (a nickname we generate, never a real name), your class, difficulty, levels, scores and XP.
  • Leaderboards show only the nickname, the character and the XP. Players under 18 appear on them only after a parent or guardian approves them from their own account.
  • Accounts are for people aged 13 and over. Children under 13 play as guests.

Counting visits

We count page views per day and taps on "I'm interested". The counts have no names, no cookies and no IP addresses. To stop abuse we keep a short-lived, scrambled code made from your connection that changes every day and can't be turned back into an address.

Notifications on Telegram

Only if you log in with Telegram and choose Telegram for notifications. You can switch back any time.

Deleting your data

Delete your account any time from your profile, or see Delete my data.
