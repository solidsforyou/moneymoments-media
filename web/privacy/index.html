Privacy

Applies to OpenSource Everything and its apps: Tomorrow, OpenPrep and GoodHabit. Draft for launch; a lawyer reviews it before we go live.

What we store

  • Your account: name, ID, age, a hashed password (we can't read it), and your app IDs.
  • If you log in with Telegram: your Telegram user ID, name and username. Never your phone number or your Telegram chats.
  • What you send and post: messages, files you send, posts and likes.

What we never do

  • No ads, and no selling or sharing your data with advertisers.
  • No reading your messages to target anything.

Who can see what

  • Messages and files: only you and the people in the chat. Files sent to "My devices" are only yours.
  • Posts: anyone on Tomorrow. Like counts are only shown to the person who posted.

Notifications on Telegram

Only if you log in with Telegram and choose Telegram for notifications. You can switch back any time.

Deleting your data

Delete your account any time from your profile, or see Delete my data.
