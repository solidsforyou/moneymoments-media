Delete my data

You can delete your account and everything linked to it yourself, in any of our apps:

  1. Log in.
  2. Open your profile (Me in OpenSource Everything, You in Tomorrow).
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

This removes your account, your IDs and your Telegram link straight away. Can't log in? Email privacy@opensourceeverything.in from the address or Telegram account you used, and we delete it for you.
