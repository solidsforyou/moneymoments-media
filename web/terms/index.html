What this is

OpenSource Everything makes free apps. Arena is free practice for classes 5 to 12 and CAT. There are no ads.

Questions and answers

  • Arena's questions are written by us, chapter by chapter, following the NCERT books and exam patterns. They are for practice. We check every answer, but if you think one is wrong, tell us and we'll fix it.
  • Readiness scores are a guide to how much you've practised, not a promise of marks in any exam.
  • NCERT, CBSE and CAT are not connected with us and haven't reviewed Arena.

Your account

  • Accounts are for people aged 13 and over. Keep your password and recovery code safe; we can't see your password.
  • Players under 18 need a parent or guardian to approve them before they appear on leaderboards.
  • Play fair: no bots, no scripts, no sharing answers to climb a leaderboard. We can remove scores or accounts that break this.
  • You can delete your account any time from Delete my data.

Paid plans

Arena is free while we launch. If we add a paid plan, it will be a one-time payment with no auto-renew, and these terms will say exactly what it covers before anyone pays.
