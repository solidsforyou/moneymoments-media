# Privacy policy outline: <app name>

Draft for the founder's lawyer to review. Not legal advice. Fill every
section from the spec's data table and the real app (every SDK included),
in plain language a user understands. Run the humanizer skill on the final
text. Publish at a public URL and link it in the app and store listings.

1. **Who we are:** company or founder name, address, and the privacy and
   grievance contact (name or role, email).
2. **What we collect:** a table: data type, example, why we need it,
   required or optional. Include data collected automatically (device,
   crash and usage data) and by third-party SDKs.
3. **Why we use it:** each purpose in one line. No vague "to improve our
   services" without saying how.
4. **Consent:** how users give it, and how to withdraw it (where in the
   app, and by email). What stops working if they withdraw.
5. **Who we share it with:** each processor or partner (hosting,
   analytics, crash reporting, payments, SMS, email) and why. "We don't
   sell your personal data" only if true.
6. **Where it's stored:** country/region of the servers.
7. **How long we keep it:** per data type, and what happens when the user
   deletes their account (and anything kept for legal reasons, with the
   reason and duration).
8. **Your rights:** access, correction, erasure, grievance redressal,
   nominating someone to act for you; how to use each and how fast we
   answer.
9. **Children:** the minimum age, and how parental consent works if
   under-18s can use the app.
10. **Security:** what we do, briefly and honestly.
11. **Delete your account:** the in-app path and the web page link.
12. **Changes:** how users are told about changes; the date of this
    version.
