## Naming in practice

- In the app, on the home screen and in conversation: **Tomorrow**.
- In app stores, search and handles: **Tomorrow by OSE**, @tomorrow.ose.
- Say "on Tomorrow" ("post on Tomorrow"), and "Open in Tomorrow" for links.
