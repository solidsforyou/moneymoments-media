# Tomorrow: positioning (29 Oct 2026)

Founder direction: now that the ecosystem exists, the core of Tomorrow is being the easiest
place to message people and to move anything between your devices and to other people,
AirDrop-style, with Telegram as the trust layer.

## What Tomorrow is

1. **Messaging that just works.** Chats with friends; your own devices show up as
   contacts too ("My laptop", "My phone").
2. **Send anything, anywhere.** Drop a file, photo, link or note on a person or a device
   and it arrives. Phone to laptop, laptop to a friend. Direct between devices when they
   can see each other (peer-to-peer), relayed when they can't.
3. **Trust from Telegram.** Log in with Telegram, so people arrive with a real, known
   identity. Notifications can go to Telegram or to Tomorrow, your choice.
4. **Social, Instagram-style.** Feed, stories, posts and a creator space, still there.
   Videos are watched in Tomorrow.
5. **Optional link to OSE.** Connect your OpenID if you want; Tomorrow works on its own.

## Product notes

- Devices pair once by scanning a code or by both being logged in; after that a device is
  just another chat.
- Transfers are end-to-end encrypted. The relay never sees file contents.
- Big files: chunked, resumable. Limits set at launch, not invented here.
- The desktop/laptop version is the website, installable as an app on Windows and Mac. A
  native desktop app is a later step (founder idea, 29 Oct).

## Tagline candidates (to run through the humanizer before use)

- Send it to yourself. Send it to anyone.
- Your phone, your laptop and your friends, in one chat.
- Drop it here. It's there.
