1 · Vision

The success engine of the new age

For ages, building things was limited to developers. Now anyone can open-source their work, and the next generation can learn, build, grow their money and prove it in one place. We are making the next gen have some good habits.

OpenID

One sign-in for everything. It shows up as PrepID inside OpenPrep and GoodID inside GoodHabit. Your profile shows what you've learnt, built and practised, with verified badges recruiters can trust.

For students

OpenPrep

The Duolingo of India for entrance exams and careers. CAT first, plus career and digital-marketing courses, taught by GoodGuy.

For builders

Community + AI Discovery Library

The GitHub of India: show and publish your work. And a library of trusted sources made for AI to search, so AI finds and recommends us.

Rules we never break

  • No ads anywhere inside our products.
  • Doomscroll useful things, never AI slop or memes. No guilt messages, no dark patterns.
  • Almost no data. Nothing sold. Training data only with opt-in, and published openly.
  • Everything that's safe to open is open: code, lessons, numbers.
  • Free, except what costs us money to run.
  • We never take a cut of donations; only the payment fee and its GST pass through.
  • GoodGuy is clearly a character. AI content is labelled.

Where it goes

  • Now: OpenPrep earns, GoodHabit and the Community grow in beta, the library seeds AI discovery.
  • Next: teachers join OpenPrep on their own once they see students paying; regional language packs; Zerodha connect.
  • Then: AccountingUnlocked for Windows and macOS, the AI Discovery API for AI developers, our own AI trained on open data.
  • The long game: chat inside GoodID grows into the WeChat of India.
2 · What launches

Four things, one website

Each product launches in the smallest form that still shows the vision. Everything lives on opensourceeverything.com so every page builds one domain's search and AI ranking, and people add it to their home screen like an app.

OpenPrep earns from day one

  • CAT: Quant, Reasoning (DILR) and Verbal, in Hinglish. About 1,000 minutes at launch: roughly 16 topics, each with 30 minutes of reels and 30 minutes of long videos, plus practice questions and one full mock.
  • Career and digital-marketing courses: 50-video courses.
  • GoodGuy teaches: the mascot beside a TV, with card animations on screen. Every number checked in code.
  • Free: 10 reels and 1 long video a day, 3 practice questions a day on career courses, 10 free minutes of AI mentoring.
  • Current affairs for GD/PI, linked to GoodHabit's news ratings.

GoodHabit beta

  • Practice investing: pretend ₹1 lakh, yesterday's-market and classic-day replays, the Ramesh, Amit and Rich guy stories, GoodGuy coaching.
  • Hissab between friends.
  • OpenPrep's reels and videos, shareable with friends.
  • A small beta of the feed, and creator applications.
  • Zerodha connect comes after API access and the legal opinion.

Community beta

  • OpenID profile: connect GitHub and show your work with a verified badge.
  • "Create a repo for me, we handle the backend": beta group first, after a security review.
  • Prep communities and student boards, Discord-style, moderated, 18+.
  • Donations to creators: UPI, GitHub Sponsors or Solana, sent straight to the creator.

AI Discovery Library v0

  • A search page for people, with our trust ratings, and readable by AI crawlers.
  • 30–50 Indian news and government sources rated by published criteria.
  • "How we rate sources" and a public corrections page.
  • llms.txt, a welcoming robots.txt, plain HTML for every page, structured data.
  • Honest links into OpenPrep, GoodHabit and the Community; never hidden instructions to AI.
3 · Profitable from day one

Every sale makes money; free stays capped

"Profitable from day one" here means three things: every paid product earns more than it costs from the first sale, fixed costs stay tiny, and the free tier has a hard daily cap so a surge of free users can't sink us. Why it works

Content is made once with archetypes and reused, so a course costs almost nothing per extra sale. Web-only payments avoid app-store cuts. AI runs on the cheapest model that does the job.

Free-tier guardrail

A free user at the full daily cap costs about ₹10–33 a month in video (480p for free long videos). Most use far less. If free streaming ever passes a set share of revenue, the daily caps tighten automatically.

No content salaries

GoodGuy and the in-house AI teachers make the launch content. Teachers join later on commission, so supply grows without fixed cost.

7 · Build plan

About two weeks to a live beta

Code is quick with Claude. Content and its correctness take the time, and payments switch on once the company's gateway account is active. Free features can go live first.

Days 1–3

Paperwork, kit and skeleton

Company registration started, domain bought, trademark filings submitted (™ can be used once filed). GoodGuy design, the TV set, style guide and the first archetypes. Website skeleton with OpenID.

Days 4–8

Products and content

OpenPrep lessons, reels, practice questions and the mock; the first career course. GoodHabit beta (practice, stories, Hissab). Community profiles and boards. Library v0 with 30–50 rated sources. About 1,000 minutes of video, every number checked in code.

Days 9–12

Review and safety

Watch every video once, fix errors. Security review of sign-in, payments and GitHub access. Load the free-tier caps and the cost guardrail.

Days 13–14

Launch the beta

Live on opensourceeverything.com. Paid plans switch on when the payment gateway is active. Build-in-public post on day one.

Weeks 3–8

Grow

Weekly content drops, Meta ads and creator waves measured by cost per sign-up, English versions, AI search checks each month.
