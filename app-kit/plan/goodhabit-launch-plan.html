1 · The idea

One founder, one company, two products

You

The face. Building in public: code, users, money and mistakes, shared weekly. "Open source everything" is already how you work.

Company

OpenSource Everything

The umbrella and the learning website. Every guide shows how GoodHabit is built, with real code and real costs.

Products

SOLIDS FOR YOU + GoodHabit

SOLIDS FOR YOU brings builders, contributors and investors, and pays your own way: your income comes from the traction GoodHabit sends to it. GoodHabit brings everyday users and revenue.

What we promise

  • Open code. GoodHabit under AGPL: anyone can read it, and anyone who runs a changed copy must share their changes. SOLIDS FOR YOU stays MIT because spreading it is the point.
  • No ads, no promotions. Brands can post their journey, never "buy now".
  • Almost no data. No tracking of what you watch, no profile of you, nothing sold. Hissab and the practice account live on your phone.
  • No algorithm. The feed is people you follow, newest first, plus an optional "Surprise me" with random posts from your topics. Spam and scams are filtered out; nothing is ranked.

What we keep private, and why

  • Users' data. India's data protection law requires it. We publish exactly what we store.
  • Keys and secrets. Zerodha and server keys never go in the repo.
  • Scam-filter word lists. We explain how the filter works, but scammers would write around published rules.
  • Security problems until fixed. Fix first, then publish what happened.
  • Contract terms with Zerodha or investors, when a contract says so. That a deal exists stays public.

Put this list on the website on day one: "everything that's safe to open, is open."

Names to check before spending on the brand

"GoodHabit" is two everyday words and close to existing habit-tracker apps. Search IP India's register (software and finance classes) before the logo, domain and handle. Pick singular "GoodHabit" everywhere: app, domain, Instagram, Play Store. Register the company as "OpenSource Everything Private Limited" with two backup names ready for the government's name check.

2 · Product

Five tabs, GoodGuy and the practice account

Everything below is clickable in the preview with sample data. Nothing in it is connected to real money yet.

Feed

Posts and reels from people you follow, stories, DMs with a scam flag, Approve (repost as "good advice"). Labelled house accounts seed it. Ends with "You're all caught up".

News

Plain search, AI picks credible articles from approved sources. Every source shows its type and a public 1 to 5 trust rating; readers review articles.

Money Machine

Impulse swap ("put ₹6,499 into your future instead?"), stocks and ETFs through Zerodha, mutual funds via Zerodha Coin, and the GoodGuy practice account.

Profile

Posts, Reels, Flexes (work and things you're proud of, taken on trust), Tagged, and Approved: a library of advice you vouched for. A good-habits graph instead of a job title.

Hissab

Splitwise-style splitting by Good ID or phone, groups, "Just me" spends and your own spending total. No daily cap. Exact to the paisa. UPI settle-up later.

GoodGuy paid

An AI teacher. Explains why a stock moved, with sources, never predicting or advising. Coaches how you trade in the practice account.

Practice account

  • Yesterday: yesterday's session replayed at speed with pretend ₹1 lakh. GoodGuy coaches habits: one stock too big, no stop-loss, panic selling, chasing, too many trades, then an end-of-day review.
  • Classic day: an old crash day (data over three months old), so GoodGuy can say exactly what a disciplined investor would do.

3 · Launch order

Launch what needs no permission first

Hissab and the practice stories need no broker, no licensed live data and no SEBI opinion, because the stories use historical or sample prices. That gets real users in months instead of after a year of paperwork.

Phase 1 · month 1–2

Hissab and practice stories

Waitlist site, public repo, weekly build-in-public posts. Open beta on the Play Store in month 2.

Phase 2 · month 3–4

GoodGuy coaching, paid trial

Practice coaching and replays of old crash days. Price test: ₹199 against ₹299. The "why it moved" explainer waits for the legal opinion.

Phase 3 · month 5–6

Text and photo feed

Follows, Approve, Flexes, News search with source ratings. Zerodha connect if API access and the legal opinion are cleared.

Later

Reels, UPI settle-up, yesterday's replay

Reels only when the money can carry the video bill (see Costs). Yesterday's replay needs licensed previous-day data and the lawyer's sign-off.

Your income is separate

You earn from SOLIDS FOR YOU, fed by the attention building GoodHabit in public brings. So the whole ₹5 lakh goes to GoodHabit. Track it apart: if SOLIDS income runs low, cut the creator waves first, never the buffer.

Three rules for the money

Spend in waves and never the whole creator budget before you know your cost per install. Never buy fake installs or followers: for an OpenSource Everything brand, being caught would end it. Only put in money you can afford to lose; most startups fail, even good ones.

6 · Money

GoodGuy pays the bills; referrals are a bonus

Zerodha's referral share pays 10% of the brokerage your users generate. Investing for the long term costs ₹0 brokerage at Zerodha, so the users GoodHabit wants earn nothing that way. The subscription is the money you control.

Competitors: Tickertape Pro ₹399; StockGro paid plans shown at ₹200–550; a practice-only app ₹125; Varsity free.

Zerodha referral share Checked

  • Back since 1 June 2026: 10% of the brokerage referred users generate, plus 300 reward points (not cash) per account.
  • Brokerage is ₹0 on delivery and direct mutual funds; intraday and F&O pay at most ₹20 an order, so at most ₹2 to us.
  • Referrals from before June keep paying only if the referrer adds 3 new ones every 12 months.
  • The post describes customers referring friends. NSE's August 2024 circular says anyone referring clients must be an Authorised Person. Ask Zerodha in writing whether an app can take part.

Pricing plan

  • Lead with coaching, the job no competitor covers.
  • Test ₹199 and ₹299 against ₹500 before fixing the price.
  • Decide a free tier: rivals give some AI for free (StockGro: 50K tokens a week).
  • Never charge for advice. GoodGuy explains and coaches; it never says what to buy in a real account.

9 · Zerodha and investors

Let traction bring them to us

The plan is open source, then traction, then investors come to us. Zerodha is the natural partner: no ads, wary of over-trading and finfluencers, and its investment arm Rainmatter has put about ₹1,500 crore into 160+ startups.

Ask Zerodha for, in this order

  • The free Kite Connect plan for startups building mass-retail products.
  • A written answer on whether an app can use the referral program.
  • An introduction to Rainmatter, once there are real users.

No public Rainmatter application page was found; it usually comes through an introduction. Keep the preview private until you present it, since it uses Zerodha's name.

What they'll ask

  • How do you make money? GoodGuy subscription first, referrals second.
  • Will it push people into F&O? No: practice before real money, coaching, no tips.
  • Is it legal? The lawyer's one-page opinion.
  • Why would users come? Waitlist, beta users, retention.
  • Who is building it? You, in public, with the code open.
What investors look at

Not GitHub stars. Weekly active users, the share still using it after 30 days, connected Zerodha accounts and the share who pay. Count these in aggregate, without tracking individuals, and publish them openly; that fits building in public.
