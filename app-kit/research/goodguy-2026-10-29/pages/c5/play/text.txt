India’s 💥💥💥 FREE 💥💥💥 NSE Stock, Stock Derivaives, Index FnO - Virtual Trading app or Paper trading app with Real Market Data.
Virtual Trading for NSE – Paper Trading with Real Market Data

Practice trading Indian markets without risking real money. This app lets you trade virtually using live market data to learn, test strategies, and build confidence before investing in the real market.

💥💥💥 VIRTUAL TRADING PLATFORM IN PLAYSTORE💥💥💥

Over 3.5 million downloads

Idea of having this platform is to enable "Anybody can trade NSE Stocks, Stock derivatives, Indicies futures and options".

Virtually Trade on the NSE Stock trading in India and their Option Chain.

This app uses live markets and their data to help beginners make their first step in the world of stock investment. Learn trading with our app and use it as a guide for your future investments.

Keep in mind this app is not a simulation and works with real market data to give you as close to real life results as possible.

One of the advantages of this app is that it let's you test your strategies before you jump into actual trading. If you are new to investment, and think you have what it takes to be investing into NSE Stock Market, then I suggest you try this app first before investing your real money. In this simulator, everything is virtual, therefore nothing to lose.

This app is for NSE stock market investors - both new and experienced. Investors new to stock market can try out the market without investing real money. Investors that are experienced can use this app to devise a strategy that works for them. Regardless of your experience with the market, you have intuition about certain stocks but are too afraid to invest; try it out here and test out your instincts.

Please understand that this is a Beta version, and we have many more exciting features coming shortly.
