About Virtual Trading 2.0
Free Paper Trading App Built for Traders Who Learn Without Losing.

Virtual Trading 2.0 is a free stock market simulator that helps you practice trading with real-time market data and virtual money — so you can build skill, test strategies, and gain confidence with zero financial risk.

Our mission
Help Traders Master the Market with Virtual Trading.

Learning to trade with real money is expensive. Most people lose capital while they learn, and by the time they develop a strategy that works, the cost of education has already wiped out their account.

We believe practice should come before risk. Traders need access to real market conditions, real price data, and real order mechanics — without the financial exposure that turns learning into gambling.

Virtual Trading 2.0 was built to make that possible. We provide live data from NSE, BSE, and MCX, virtual currency that behaves like the real thing, and the full suite of trading tools — options, futures, and equities — so you can practice as if it were real, because it feels real.

Our mission is simple: help traders build skill, test strategies, and enter the market with confidence through paper trading — not capital at risk.

What we value
Principles That Shape the Platform.

Six commitments that guide how we build, who we build for, and what we choose not to do.

Practice First

We focus on tools that improve real trading skills. Not flashy features that look impressive but don't make you a better trader.

Simplicity Matters

Learning to trade should feel clear, approachable, and trustworthy. Complexity is a barrier — we remove it wherever we can.

Built for Impact

Every feature should help you trade smarter, learn faster, or build real confidence. We measure outcomes, not feature counts.

Reliability Over Hype

We care more about consistent, accurate market data and stable tools than trends, noise, or inflated promises. The platform has to hold up.

Trader Centered

We design around how traders actually operate — with real market hours, real strategies, and real learning curves on the line.

Long Term Thinking

We build for lasting skill development so traders can practice with confidence, not chase one-off simulations or short-lived setups.
