Practice F&O, options, futures and commodities on live NSE, BSE and MCX prices. No real money at risk. Strategy Builder*, Basket Orders, Option Chain, SL, TGT, TSL*.

Practice the Indian stock market, F&O, options, futures and commodity trading with virtual funds, without placing real-money orders.

ZeroStake is a paper trading and virtual trading simulator for Indian markets. Explore stocks, options, futures and commodities across NSE, BSE and MCX while practicing orders, strategies and market analysis in a simulated environment.

PAPER TRADING WITH VIRTUAL FUNDS

Practice intraday, delivery, F&O and commodity trading with virtual money. Set custom virtual capital up to Rs. 10 Crore.*

ADVANCED ORDERS & TRADE SIMULATION

Practice with:

* Market and Limit Orders*
* Stop Loss (SL) and Target (TGT)
* Trailing Stop Loss (TSL)*
* GTT Orders with advanced trigger controls*
* AMO Orders*
* Intraday and Delivery simulation
* Basket Orders*
* Exit All for simulated positions*

TRADE DIRECTLY FROM CHARTS

Place simulated trades from charts across stocks, indices and commodities. Set Stop Loss and Target visually. Faster chart updates and additional chart-trading controls are available with ZeroStake PRO.*

OPTION CHAIN

Explore CE and PE Option Chain data for Nifty 50, Bank Nifty, Sensex and FinNifty. Analyze Open Interest, OI Change, LTP, Implied Volatility (IV), Volume and strike-wise market data.

OPTIONS STRATEGY BUILDER*

Build and simulate multi-leg options strategies including Iron Condor, Bull Call Spread, Bear Put Spread, Straddle and Strangle. Review legs, payoff, breakeven and risk before placing a simulated Basket Order.

MARKET ANALYSIS

View Market Heatmap across NSE, BSE and MCX instruments. Track FII/DII activity and Advance-Decline Ratio. Detailed Market Depth* shows bid, ask, available quantity and liquidity.

TRADE JOURNAL & P&L REVIEW

Review simulated activity with Trade Journal, trade history, P&L statements, and position and order history.

REPLAY TRADING

Practice with historical market sessions. Replay past price action, place simulated trades and explore different decisions.

BUILT FOR INDIAN MARKET PRACTICE

ZeroStake combines Paper Trading, Virtual Trading, F&O Trading, Options Trading, Futures Trading, Commodity Trading, Option Chain, Strategy Builder*, Basket Orders*, GTT Orders*, Chart Trading, Market Heatmap, FII/DII Data, Market Depth*, Trade Journal, Replay Trading and Virtual P&L for NSE, BSE and MCX markets.

IMPORTANT SIMULATION & RISK DISCLOSURE

ZeroStake is a virtual trading simulation tool. No real securities or commodity orders are transmitted to or executed on an exchange through ZeroStake. Virtual funds and simulated profits have no cash value.

ZeroStake does not provide investment advice, research recommendations, trading calls, stock tips, price targets, guaranteed returns or portfolio-management services. ZeroStake is not registered with SEBI as a stock broker, Investment Adviser or Research Analyst.

Simulated fills, P&L and results may differ from real-market execution due to liquidity, spreads, slippage, latency, partial fills, rejected orders, brokerage, taxes and margin requirements. Simulated performance is hypothetical and is not indicative of future results.

Information in ZeroStake is for simulation and informational purposes only and is not a recommendation to buy, sell or hold any security. ZeroStake is not affiliated with or endorsed by SEBI, NSE, BSE or MCX.

* Certain features, advanced controls and higher usage limits require ZeroStake PRO. Some starred features may also be available with limited functionality on the free plan.

ZeroStake is free to download and use. ZeroStake PRO is optional. The monthly plan is auto-renewable; the yearly plan is one-time and non-renewing. Prices are shown before purchase. Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation. Manage or cancel the monthly plan in your App Store account settings. The yearly plan does not renew automatically.
