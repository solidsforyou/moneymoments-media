About this app
arrow_forward
Research & invest in Indian & US stocks, ETFs, and mutual funds with powerful stock screener, forecast, scorecard & price alert tools. Invest in US stocks & ETFs from India, starting at just $1.

Track & analyse all your investments in one place – connect multiple brokers and MF accounts (PANs), check your diversification score, spot red flags & forecast potential returns – with an all-in-one portfolio tracker.

Ask Portfolio AI anything about your investments, or connect Claude, ChatGPT & more via Tickertape MCP to buy & sell US stocks from your chatbot.

INVEST IN US STOCKS & ETFs
Invest in 10,000+ US stocks & ETFs from India, with a dedicated US stock screener to help you choose. Access Netflix, Spotify, Meta & other global market leaders through the RBI-compliant GIFT City route. Diversify across the NASDAQ 100 & S&P 500, starting with just $1, through fractional shares.

STOCK ANALYSIS & INSIGHTS
Stay ahead in the stock market with live stock prices, indices, charts & stock scorecards. Use research tools like a stock screener, bulk stock deals, real-time stock news, share price alerts, AI-powered stock sentiment analysis & stock forecasts. Get daily updates on NIFTY, SENSEX, corporate actions, FII/DII data, and more.

STOCK SCREENER
Analyse stocks & ETFs with 200+ filters or 15+ pre-built screens. Apply fundamental filters (market cap, dividend yield, P/B, P/E ratio, ROCE & more) & technical indicators (RSI, SMA, EMA) to build trading strategies.

MUTUAL FUND SCREENER
Screen mutual funds with 50+ filters & 10+ pre-built screens. Compare NAV, AUM, expense ratio, fund manager & multi-year CAGR, then invest via SIP or lump sum.

MMI (MARKET MOOD INDEX)
Spot buy or sell opportunities with MMI. Track whether market sentiment is greedy or fearful to better time your trades, lump-sum & SIP investments.

AI INVESTING ASSISTANT & MCP
Uncover hidden insights in your portfolio with Portfolio AI. Spot overlapping holdings & risks, screen stocks & funds, and research financials & valuations in seconds, in any language. Connect Tickertape to Claude, ChatGPT & more via MCP to buy & sell US stocks directly from your chatbot.

PORTFOLIO ANALYSIS
Link multiple demat accounts from top brokers like Groww, Zerodha (Kite), Angel One, Upstox, 5paisa, IIFL Capital, Motilal Oswal, Trustline & more, or upload holdings statements from INDmoney, ICICI Direct, Kotak Securities, Axis Direct & any other broker. Track Indian & US stocks, ETFs, mutual funds, digital gold, SIPs & smallcases in one portfolio tracker.

LOAN AGAINST SECURITIES
Get a loan against mutual funds and stocks without selling them, at interest rates starting at 9.9% p.a., with zero foreclosure charges.
Lenders: Bajaj Finance Limited (BFL), South Indian Bank (SIB), DSP Finance
Interest rate: Starting at 9.9% p.a.
Example: Interest is charged only on the outstanding amount. On ₹1,00,000 outstanding at 9.9% p.a., monthly interest = 1,00,000 × 9.9% ÷ 12 = ₹825. Repay principal anytime during the loan tenure.

INVEST IN SMALLCASES
Invest in smallcases – professionally managed model portfolios of IN & US stocks, ETFs & mutual funds built around themes, ideas or strategies.

INVEST IN GOLD
Buy & sell digital gold, gold ETFs & gold mutual funds. Track gold prices and diversify your asset allocation.

INVEST IN FIXED DEPOSITS
Earn fixed returns with FD interest rates up to 8% p.a. Eligible FDs are covered by DICGC insurance up to ₹5 lakh per depositor per bank.

NOTE: Equity investments are subject to market risks. Investments in global markets are subject to market, currency, regulatory, and jurisdictional risks. Read all the related documents carefully before investing. Investors should consider all risk factors and consult their financial advisors before investing. Representations are not indicative of future results.
