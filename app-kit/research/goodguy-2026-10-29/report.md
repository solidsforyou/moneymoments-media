# GoodGuy: competitor research (29 Oct 2026)

Row ids like [c1-008] point to `ledger.csv`; every row there has a source URL and capture
time. Computed tables are in `tables.md`.

## 1. Answer

Rs 500 a month is above every paid product found for the same buyer:

- Tickertape Pro: Rs 399 a month [c4-001].
- StockGro paid plans: shown at Rs 200 to 550 [c1-008, c1-011, c1-012].
- Practice-only app (Virtual Trading 2.0): Rs 125 [c5-001].
- Free: Varsity, and Sensibull for Zerodha users [c2-002, c3-003].

None of the five offers GoodGuy's mix: a cited, non-advisory "why it moved", replay practice
and coaching on the user's own trading. There is little evidence on what users will pay, so
Rs 500 is a bet, not a finding. A launch price of Rs 199 to 299 fits the evidence better (that
part is judgement, not data).

## 3. Competitors

### c1 StockGro (stockgro.club)

- **Size:** claims 35M+ users [c1-005, vendor claim]; 1Cr+ Play downloads [c1-017].
- **Price per month:**
  - Basic: free.
  - Advanced: Rs 499 listed, Rs 200 shown.
  - Expert: Rs 699 listed, Rs 450 shown.
  - Stoxo PRO: Rs 999 listed, Rs 550 shown [c1-008, c1-011, c1-012, c1-013].
  - The page says "20% Off With Assured Cashback", which doesn't match the gap between the
    listed and shown prices, so which price buyers pay is unclear.
- **SEBI:** research analyst registration INH000018300, held through a subsidiary [c1-004].
- **AI:** Stoxo [c1-006]. Its example prompts are buy questions ("Should you buy ONGC or Oil
  India?") [c1-007]. The free tier is 50K tokens a week [c1-013]. No citations seen.
- **Practice trading:** its own llms.txt says it "is not a virtual trading, paper trading, or
  simulated trading platform" [c1-003].

### c2 Zerodha Varsity (zerodha.com/varsity)

- **Price:** free. "No signup, no pay-wall, no ads." [c2-002, c2-004]
- **Play:** 4.43 from 12,193 ratings [c2-006, c2-007].
- **Not found:** an AI feature, practice trading.

### c3 Sensibull (sensibull.com)

- **Price:** Pro is "100% free for Zerodha users" [c3-003, c3-005].
- **SEBI:** research analyst registration INH200006895 [c3-002].
- **Play:** 4.72 from 47,304 ratings [c3-009, c3-010].
- **Mindful Trading:** a prompt before a trade to "pause, control your emotions" [c3-007]. It is
  the closest thing found to GoodGuy's coaching, but it doesn't review how someone traded.
- **Draft Portfolios:** lets you try a strategy before placing it [c3-008].

### c4 Tickertape (tickertape.in)

- **Price:** Pro is Rs 399 a month, Rs 899 a quarter, or Rs 2,399.2 a year with a coupon
  [c4-001, c4-003, c4-004].
- **Play:** 4.44 from 70,144 ratings [c4-009, c4-010].
- **Features:** screeners, forecasts, news, and earnings-call sentiment [c4-007].
- **Not found:** practice trading.

### c5 Virtual Trading 2.0 (kavkrishapps.in)

- **Price:** free with ads; Pro is Rs 125 a month, Rs 250 a quarter or Rs 750 a year [c5-001,
  c5-003, c5-004].
- **Prices it shows:** 15 minutes delayed on the free plan, real time on Pro [c5-005, c5-006].
  How it licenses that data: not found.
- **Play:** 4.18 from 45,956 ratings [c5-009, c5-010].
- **Complaints:** ads that cost seconds in fast trades, and glitches.

## 4. Gaps GoodGuy can own

1. **Reviewing the user's own trading.** The nearest are Sensibull's pre-trade prompt [c3-007]
   and Virtual Trading 2.0's stop-loss auto-exit [c5-008].
2. **A cited "why it moved" that never advises.** StockGro's AI answers buy questions [c1-007].
3. **Replays of yesterday and of crash days.** None of the five offers them.
4. **Ad-free practice.** The incumbent's free tier has ads [c5-003].
5. **A price between Rs 125 and Rs 200.** Nothing found there except promotions.

## 5. Threats

- **Sensibull:** free for Zerodha users and already has Mindful Trading.
- **StockGro:** scale, research analyst registration, and a free AI tier.
- **Varsity:** sets the price of learning at zero.
- **Rs 125 practice apps:** cap what practice alone can charge.

## 6. Next decisions (ranked)

1. Test Rs 199 and Rs 299 against Rs 500 before fixing the price.
2. Make coaching the headline, not the explainer.
3. Check whether StockGro still offers practice trading in its app [c1-003].
4. Decide the free tier: rivals give some AI for free [c1-013].
5. Get the lawyer's opinion on price data and research analyst status. Virtual Trading 2.0
   sells real-time paper prices, and how it does that is unknown [c5-005].
