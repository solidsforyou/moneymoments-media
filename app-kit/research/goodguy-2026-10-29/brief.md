# Brief: GoodGuy competitor research (2026-10-29)

1. **Question.** Can GoodGuy, a paid AI "why did this stock move" explainer plus a replay practice account with behaviour coaching, win in India at Rs 500 per month against what Indian retail investors already use for free or pay for?
2. **Our product (id `us`).** GoodGuy, a planned paid feature inside GoodHabits (Indian, Android-first, ad-free social + investing app connected to Zerodha via Kite Connect). AI on every company page explains why a stock moved from cited news, never predicting or advising. Practice account replays yesterday's market at real speed, plus older classic replays (e.g. March 2020 crash); AI coaches the user's own behaviour (position size, stop-loss, panic selling, end-of-day review). Proposed Rs 500/month (also testing Rs 199-299). Not launched, no reviews. Source: /home/user/moneymoments-media/app-kit/brief.md and memory #69, #70.
3. **Market.** India; Google Play India (Android), web.
4. **Depth level.** Quick-plus: 5 competitors (the Deep range is 5 to 8), full pricing, Play rating, whatever reviews are reachable. Review depth is below the Deep target (see report data note).
5. **Use.** Pricing decision and positioning for GoodGuy; feeds a harsh-buyer review.

## Competitor list (5)
c1 StockGro, c2 Zerodha Varsity, c3 Sensibull, c4 Tickertape, c5 Virtual Trading 2.0. Reasons are in competitors.csv.

## Left out
- Univest: SEBI-RA advisory/trade-tip subscription with AI portfolio review; not the explain-or-practice job; its price table was empty on its own blog, so no verified plan prices.
- Groww / Upstox learning: broker-owned free education, same position as Varsity; not researched separately.
- Neostox, FrontPage, Pocketful "paper" app, AlgoTest: other paper-trading names seen in search results (including a reviewer who recommends Neostox); not researched, time-boxed.
- Tradetron, TradingView: algo or global charting, different buyer.
