# New app brief (v1)

Agreed with the founder on 28 Oct 2026 before any design. Mockups and reviews start from this.
Name: to be chosen (Claude suggests names; the name should say "everything finance, as a social network").

## What it is

A social video network for everything finance, where every video is verified, with crypto
trading built in. A trust source for serious topics, not an engagement algorithm.

## Who it's for

Everyone at once: first-time investors, active crypto traders and people who only want news
they can trust. Global from day one. The first users are mostly on iPhone.

## Content

- Topics: markets and crypto, economy and business, world and political news, personal
  finance, anything finance-related.
- Formats: short vertical videos and long explainers. No live streams in v1.
- Any spoken language; every video has English subtitles. The app interface is in English.

## Trust rules

- Only approved creators can post. Everyone else watches, reacts, comments and trades.
- Verified means all four: creator ID checked (KYC), creator credentials checked, every
  video fact-checked before it goes live, and sources cited for claims.
- Fact-checking: AI checks first and flags claims, then people review. Approved creators get a
  fast track.
- A video found wrong after going live is taken down and the creator gets a strike.

## Social (v1)

Follow, like, Approve (a viewer vouches that the video is accurate, separate from like),
comment, share/repost, DMs.

## Feed

Tabs the user switches between, e.g. Following, Top stories (picked by editors), For you.

## Trading

- Crypto only. No stocks planned yet.
- A crypto gateway partner handles custody, trades, licences and which countries can trade.
  The app is only the front end. The design still shows a "not available in your country"
  state, in case the gateway blocks a country.

## Money

- Revenue: trading fees only.
- Creators aren't paid. They come for the verified badge, the fast-track checks and the reach,
  and the in-house team makes the early videos.
- Ads: the app's own "stop scrolling and invest" cards, shown like Instagram ads but far more
  rarely. Each crypto ad carries the legal risk warning required where the user lives.

## v1 goal

A small real launch: a few hundred users and a handful of creators, on the App Store, to prove
people watch and trade.

## Risks to keep in view

- Comments and DMs are where crypto scams spread, so they need moderation and scam-link
  blocking from day one.
- A fact-check before every video goes live is the biggest running cost and slows breaking news.
- Ad rules (e.g. India's advertising standards council, ASCI): crypto ads can't play down risk
  or promise returns.
