## ElevenLabs settings (Viraj)
- Model: Multilingual v2 (handles Hinglish). Stability ~45, Similarity ~80, Style ~15-20, Speaker boost on.
- Generate one line at a time so each lands on its own shot; pick the best take of each.
- Numbers are written as spoken ("two-thirty", "nine ninety-nine") so the voice doesn't say "two hundred thirty".
- Full stops and commas are the pauses; "...aar-paar" style pauses come from the comma, not from dashes.
- Export WAV, 44.1 kHz. The mix ducks the music under the voice and lets the phonk hit full on the last line.
