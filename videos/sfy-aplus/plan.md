# SOLIDS FOR YOU: Amazon product video, plan (31 Oct 2026)

Master video workflow, stages 1 to 3 (plan only; nothing built yet). Final timings come
from the founder's ElevenLabs voice-over; the times below are estimates (~82 words of VO at
about 2.3 words a second, plus holds).

## 1. Brief (careful mode)

1. **Goal:** a shopper on the listing understands in one watch why this plain tee or jogger
   is worth Rs 999 / Rs 1,499: 230 / 320 GSM, 100% cotton, made in India, no itchy tag,
   colour holds, and how it fits.
2. **Audience and where:** Amazon.in shoppers, 18-30, on a phone. The video autoplays
   **muted** in the image block, so every point must work on mute (big on-screen text).
3. **Placement (founder to confirm):** the detail-page product video (Seller Central:
   Manage videos), one video attached to all 6 listings. A+ video needs Premium A+; check
   if "Manage A+ content" offers it.
4. **Format:** 16:9, 1920x1080, 30 fps, MP4 (H.264, AAC), about 45 s.
5. **Amazon video rules to respect (from experience, not checked against the current
   guideline page; check it when uploading):** no price, discount or "sale"; no "best" or
   "#1"; no reviews or ratings; no URL, phone or email; no competitor names; nothing
   time-sensitive; every claim must match the listing.
6. **Claims allowed (confirmed facts only):** 230 GSM tees, 320 GSM joggers, 100% cotton,
   made in India, printed neck label (no tag), colour held after 5+ washes (founder's own
   test), pre-shrunk fabric, S/M/L, oversized: pick one size down for a regular fit, joggers
   wide leg with two deep pockets. No comparison numbers about other brands.
7. **Voice-over:** Hinglish, plain words, clear for tier-2/3 without sounding cheap to tier-1
   (memory #152), made by the founder in ElevenLabs (#8). Script below, Humanizer 100/100.
8. **Done means:** motion-design-taste 85+ with nothing under 7; audio clean at -14 LUFS,
   true peak -1.5 dBTP; no serious objection left from a harsh buyer review; careful-mode
   pass on the final file.

## 2. Style spec (matches the Brand Story)

- **Palette:** near-black paper oklch(13% 0.012 264) (the ground), ink oklch(96% 0.008 264)
  (text), brand blue oklch(58% 0.21 264) (the one accent: rules and the key number),
  soft blue oklch(80% 0.09 264) (small labels). No other colours.
- **Type at 1920x1080:** Barlow Condensed 900 uppercase for heads (140 to 220 px,
  line-height 1.0); Manrope 600/700 for captions (44 to 56 px). Captions sit in the lower
  third, max 7 words, appear with the words.
- **Grid:** 96 px margins; text left-aligned on the left third, footage on the right or
  full-bleed behind a dark shade, as on the Brand Story cards.
- **Motion:** slow push-ins on every shot (100% to 106% over the shot); text enters with
  an 8 px rise and fade over 9 frames, exits in 6; groups stagger 3 frames; cuts on the VO
  beat; one cross-dissolve only, into the end card. No whip-pans: calm, premium.
- **Sound:** a calm music bed from the licensed library (music-calm or music-beds),
  ducked 12 dB under the voice; soft transition whooshes on 3 cuts at most; one soft
  logo sting at the end. -14 LUFS.
- **Footage:** real SFY clips (assets/sfy/clips; the 4K cutting clip from Drive), the
  Brand Story photos, the six product cutouts (assets/sfy/products). The short clips (about
  1 s) are slowed to 30-40% speed.
