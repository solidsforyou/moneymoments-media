Zerodha (@zerodhaonline) never raised outside money. In FY25 it made ₹4,237 crore in profit on ₹8,847 crore of revenue.

Zerodha's business model, explained: that's about ₹48 profit on every ₹100 of revenue, a margin most startups never reach. Profit did fall from ₹5,496 crore in FY24, and CEO Nithin Kamath said brokerage revenue was down about 40% in April to June 2025 compared with a year earlier. FY26 profit was ₹4,283 crore on flat revenue.

No VC, no IPO, just profit. Could a startup like this be built today?

Sources: Entrackr (FY25 consolidated figures from RoC filings, cash of ₹22,679 crore, brokerage drop). YourStory (Aug 2026, FY26 profit).
