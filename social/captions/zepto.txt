The Zepto IPO story in 60 seconds. Zepto was started in 2021 by two 19-year-old Stanford dropouts to deliver groceries in 10 minutes. In FY26 it made ₹22,624 crore in revenue and lost ₹5,905 crore: about ₹26 lost for every ₹100 of revenue. Its listing, planned for August 2026, is now aimed at February to May 2027.
