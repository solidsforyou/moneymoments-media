Meesho charges sellers zero commission. So how does it make money?

Meesho's business model, explained: logistics fees and ads that sellers pay for to be seen. In April to September 2025 its revenue grew 29% to ₹5,578 crore, and its loss shrank to ₹701 crore from ₹2,513 crore a year earlier. Then came the IPO: issue price ₹111, opening price ₹162.50 on 10 December 2025, a 46% premium, after being subscribed about 79 times.
