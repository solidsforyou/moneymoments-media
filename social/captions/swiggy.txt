Swiggy's share fell as much as 17% last week. But on every ₹100 of food you order, how much does Swiggy actually keep? About ₹3.40.

Swiggy's July to September quarter, explained: from July to September 2026, people ordered ₹10,054 crore of food on Swiggy, and the food business kept ₹341 crore after delivery, staff and marketing costs (adjusted EBITDA), 3.4% of order value. Instamart, its quick-commerce arm, lost ₹895 crore on the same measure, up from ₹778 crore the quarter before. In the same quarter, rival Blinkit made ₹184 crore (the two companies don't define the measure in exactly the same way). Swiggy's total loss rose to ₹922 crore from ₹791 crore, and the share hit a 52-week low on 23 October.
