Your loan rate just went up. Did your bank quietly add a year?

After the RBI's 0.25% hike, take a ₹30 lakh, 20-year home loan going from 8.50% to 8.75%. You have two ways to absorb it.

Option 1, pay a higher EMI: ₹26,035 becomes ₹26,511, about ₹477 more a month. That's ₹1.14 lakh extra over the loan.

Option 2, keep the EMI and stretch the loan: it runs about 12 months longer, and you pay ₹3.24 lakh extra.

Same loan, about ₹2 lakh apart. So ask your bank for a higher EMI, not a longer loan, if your budget allows. RBI rules from August 2023 say that when a floating rate resets, you can pick a higher EMI or a longer tenure, or mix the two. The lender has to tell you about any change.

This is a worked example with illustrative rates. It assumes the full hike is passed on. Check your own lender's terms. Not financial advice.

Source: RBI circular on reset of floating interest rates on EMI loans (18 August 2023). Maths: standard EMI formula.
