D2C boom khatam? Not quite. The money didn't vanish, the big cheques did.

India's D2C brands, the ones you find on Instagram first, raised $1.6 billion in 2022. By 2024 that was down to $824 million, and 2025 came in at $898 million, about 44% below the peak. Late-stage rounds, the big cheques for grown-up brands, fell from $883 million to $271 million, a 69% drop. Now 70% of the money goes to new, small brands at seed and early stage, up from 38% in 2021.
