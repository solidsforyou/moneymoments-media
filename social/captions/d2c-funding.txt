Is India's D2C boom dying? Here's the hisab.

Every boom starts with outside money. Money buys ads, ads buy views, views bring more brands. In 2022, Indian D2C brands raised $1.6 billion. Then the money pulled back: $824 million in 2024, $898 million in 2025. The big late-stage cheques fell 69%, from $883 million to $271 million. Now 70% of the money goes to tiny, early-stage brands, up from 38% in 2021.

Our take: small cheques fund copies, not new ideas. Feeds are full of Meesho-style resellers and ₹299 lookalikes. The next D2C boom won't come from more money. It'll come from better products.
