Urban Company's IPO was 103 times oversubscribed and listed at a 57% premium. Now read the fine print.

The Urban Company profit story, explained: FY25 revenue grew 38% to ₹1,144.5 crore and net profit was ₹240 crore, its first full-year profit. But ₹211 crore of that was a deferred tax credit, an accounting entry rather than money from customers. One analysis puts profit without it at about ₹28.5 crore. In April to June 2025 it made ₹6.9 crore on ₹367 crore of revenue.
