Prices just hit a 2-year high. Your EMI is next.

Retail inflation rose to 5.54% in September 2026, up from 4.83% in August and 2.75% in January. It's the highest since October 2024. Food is hit the hardest at 7.74%, as a weak monsoon pushed up vegetable prices and crude oil stayed costly. So on 7 October the RBI raised the repo rate, the rate banks borrow at, from 5.25% to 5.50%. It's the first hike since 2023.

What it means for you: EMIs on floating-rate loans go up, fixed deposit rates may rise, and groceries stay costly.
