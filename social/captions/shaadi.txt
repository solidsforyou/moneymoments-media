Shaadi.com (@shaadi.com) is free to join. So who pays?

How India's biggest matrimony site makes money: it started as Sagaai.com in 1997 and became Shaadi.com in 1999. You can make a profile, search and send interest for free. To see a match's phone number, chat without limits or video call, you need Premium. That's the business: love is free, the phone number isn't.

In FY22 it reported ₹261 crore in revenue and a ₹13 crore loss. Newer numbers aren't widely reported.
