The Lenskart (@lenskart) IPO was 28 times oversubscribed. Then it listed below its issue price.

The Lenskart IPO story: issue price ₹402, NSE listing at ₹395 on 10 November 2025. The reason most analysts gave was valuation, with price-to-earnings estimates from 235x to 285x against about 50x for global peer EssilorLuxottica. FY25 profit was ₹297 crore, but about ₹167 crore of it was a one-time gain. It closed day one at ₹404.55 on NSE, just above the issue price.

Hype isn't valuation. Would you have applied?
