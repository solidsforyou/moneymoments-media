# Startup page strategy (draft, 25 Oct 2026)

Assumptions to confirm: one person makes everything, the page is
faceless (typography, graphics, own photos), Instagram first.

## 1. Goal and metric (90 days)
Build an Indian startup page that young Indians with spending money follow,
and send them to Solids For You. The number that counts: **DMs and site
visits from SFY reels**. Followers matter only as the route to that.

## 2. Audience
Indian, 21-30, in college or early career, in metros and tier-2 cities.
Reads Inc42 headlines, watches Shark Tank India clips, has thought about
starting something. Wants to understand how money moves in Indian startups
and wants to feel early. Afraid of falling behind, and of being sold to.
Scrolls Instagram Reels in English and Hinglish.

## 3. Platforms
Instagram Reels only for the first 90 days. YouTube Shorts can follow with
the same files once the system holds. Not doing yet: X, LinkedIn, TikTok
(not available in India).

## 4. Positioning
"How Indian startups really make money, explained in 30 seconds, by people
building one."
The SFY reels are that story told from the inside: **building Solids For
You in public**. That keeps them on topic instead of looking like ads.

## 5. Honesty rules for this page
- The bio says who runs it, for example "By the team building
  @solidsforyou". Promoting your own brand from a page that looks
  independent breaks the Social Media rules (no astroturfing) and India's
  ASCI disclosure guidelines (check the current text).
- Every startup number comes from a public source (annual report, MCA
  filing, DRHP, credible press). Put the source on screen or in the caption.
  No source, no number.
- No ripped clips. Shark Tank India, podcast and news footage need
  permission, or copyright strikes will kill the page. Use original
  typography, graphics and your own photos.
- No investment tips and no "guaranteed" income claims.

## 6. Content pillars

| # | Pillar (series name) | Job | Example working hook |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Startup Math | Reach + trust | "Here's what a quick-commerce app keeps from your ₹10 delivery fee" (only with sourced figures) |
| 2 | Decoded (news) | Reach, reactive | "This startup just raised money. Here's what it actually means" |
| 3 | 30-Second Founder | Reach | Public founder story, one turning point per reel |
| 4 | Brand Autopsy | Trust | "Why this D2C brand won and this one died" (sourced) |
| 5 | Your First ₹1 Lakh | Reach (Gen Z money and careers) | "ESOPs explained before you sign that offer" (education only, no advice) |
| 6 | Building SFY (SFY reels) | Convert | "Day 1 of building a clothing brand. Here's the math" |

Every reel goes through the SOLIDS FOR YOU tools, routed by Master: the
Social Media scorecard and Humanizer on every script and caption, Harsh Buyer
Review on SFY reels, and the Design head's video workflow (with Motion
Design Taste) for anything rendered. SFY product facts come only from
`memory/memory.csv`; none are recorded yet.

## 7. Cadence: work up to 9 a day, don't start there

| Weeks | Startup reels/day | SFY reels/day | Reels/week |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1-2 | 2 | 1 | 21 |
| 3-4 | 4 | 1 | 35 |
| 5+ | 6 | 1 to 3 | 49 to 63 |

Go up a step only if the last two weeks shipped every planned reel and
average watch time didn't fall. 9 reels a day is 63 a week, a full-time job
or more for one person. Start SFY at 1 a day so promotion stays a small share
of the page, and raise it if DMs keep coming.

## 8. How to keep up the volume
- One editing template per series (same layout, fonts, colours, end card).
  Each new reel then only needs new text, numbers and voiceover.
- One research block a week builds a bank of sourced facts. Each fact
  becomes one reel.
- Daily slots stay the same (series A at 9am, B at 1pm, and so on) so
  you aren't deciding every day what to post.
- Everything is prepared ready to post. Nothing is published automatically
  unless the founder says so.

## 9. Test first
1. Hinglish vs English voiceover on the same Startup Math reel.
2. A number on frame 0 vs a question on frame 0.
3. Which series sends the most profile visits to SFY.
Log every reel in `social/posts.csv` and review weekly.
