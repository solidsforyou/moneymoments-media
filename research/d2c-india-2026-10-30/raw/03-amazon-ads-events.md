## Key takeaways

1. **Amazon's own India ad revenue is large and growing.** Amazon India ad income was about Rs 8,342-8,370 crore in FY25, up roughly 24-25% (press coverage of regulatory filings). [B] (exchange4media, storyboard18, socialsamosa; 2025)
2. **No authoritative India CPC or ACoS benchmark was found.** Every India number I found is from a vendor or agency blog, and the figures conflict. Treat them as directional only. [C]
3. **Amazon's own ranking statement:** ad rank is a score from relevance and bid, and relevance has been weighted more heavily over time. Amazon says about 92% of selected Sponsored Products ads in 2024 were not the highest bid. This is a US-auction statement, but the mechanism is the same product. [A/B, see note on source] (ppc.land / Amazon statement, dated 2024-2025)
4. **Relevance is not published.** Vendors describe it as click, conversion and listing signals. These formulas are vendor interpretations, not Amazon documentation. [C]
5. **Sponsored Products run on Amazon.in** with Automatic, Manual Keyword and Manual Product campaign types. [C, several agency guides, 2025-2026]
6. **AMC (Amazon Marketing Cloud) was opened to all Sponsored Ads advertisers, including India,** per Amazon's own India-language launch note dated 18 Sep 2025 (self-serve, no stated spend minimum). [A] (advertising.amazon.com hi-in, 2025-09-18)
7. **Amazon Ads AI tools in India:** AI image generator (beta late 2024, lifestyle images); AI video generator (announced for India Nov 2025, free through Creative Studio, builds Sponsored Brands video from product images or detail pages); Creative Agent and Ads Agent (Connected Worlds India, March 2026). [A/B; the Nov 2025 date rests on trade press]
8. **Prime Day India 2025 ran 12-14 July 2025**, a 72-hour, first three-day edition, described as Prime-members-only in most sources. [B/A, several outlets citing Amazon press, June 2025]
9. **Prime Day India 2026 is reported as 4-6 July 2026**, with a Prime Shopping Days follow-on from 7 July. Sources conflict on whether non-Prime shoppers could take part. This is aggregator-level; I could not confirm it on Amazon's own page. [C/B, 2026]
10. **Great Indian Festival 2026 reported to start 8 Oct 2026** (Prime members from 7 Oct per one report). Several outlets agree. No end date and no seller or GMV numbers were found for 2026. [B, 2026, multiple outlets]
11. **Great Indian Festival 2025: Amazon's headline numbers are visits, not GMV.** 276 crore customer visits (about 2.76 billion); the most SMB seller participation in the first 48 hours; over two-thirds of those SMB sellers from tier 2 and 3 cities; record local-shop sellers crossing Rs 10 lakh in sales; Amazon Bazaar sellers crossing Rs 1 lakh up 15x year on year. [A/B; sourced via search snippets citing Amazon press releases, Oct 2025]
12. **Amazon's own 2025 GIF GMV figure was not found.** The only GMV number (Rs 25,000 crore in the first two days, +25% YoY) is at market level, not Amazon-only. [B, Deccan Chronicle, 2025]
13. **Referral fees on low-priced items were cut.** Zero referral fee for products under Rs 300 from 7 Apr 2025 (Amazon press, Aug/Mar 2025). A later report says the zero-fee threshold moved to under Rs 1,000 from 16 Mar 2026 (single source, B). This is a fee change, not a deal or ads fee.
14. **Influencer programme is large and India-specific in parts.** About 2 lakh creators by Oct 2026 (reported doubled YoY). Tech Influencer Programme gives up to 20 days early access to launches. Elevate is invite-only and India-only. No application fee. Minimum about 2,000 followers with engagement thresholds. [B/A, 2025-2026]
15. **Brand Referral Bonus (BRB) for Amazon.in: no evidence found that it is live.** Every source I found describes it as US-only and brand-registered. The Hindi Amazon Ads launch page does not settle India availability. [C; unresolved]
16. **Lightning Deal fee rules for India: not found from an official source.** US fee rules (daily fee plus a share of sales, Prime Day flat fees) are documented in seller forums. An India-focused blog gives Rs 1,000-5,000 for a Lightning Deal with a 45-day lead time, but it cites nothing, so treat it as unverified. [C, unverified]
17. **Coupon and deal eligibility thresholds** (e.g. Professional plan, 3.5-star rating, 5-review minimum) come from seller-consultancy blogs and are not confirmed for India. [C, unverified]

## Findings by sub-question

### 1. Ad formats on Amazon.in (Sponsored Products / Brands / Display / video)

- Sponsored Products run on Amazon India search results and product pages, with Automatic, Manual Keyword and Manual Product campaign types. [C, agency guides e.g. evanik.ai, growthjockey, 2025-2026; URLs in Sources]
- **Sponsored TV** (streaming TV, starting with Amazon MX Player) launched for sellers on Amazon.in, March 2025. [B, storyboard18 / exchange4media, Mar 2025]
- **Sponsored Brands video** can be made with Amazon's AI Video Generator, which Amazon's India launch note lists as available. Amazon's page says "open beta". Coverage says it is free through Creative Studio. [A/B, Nov 2025; the exact date rests on trade press]
- **Prime Video formats:** new Interactive Video Ads and Pause Ads for India, announced Oct 2026. [B, exchange4media, 2026; details not verified]
- **Display ads:** no India-specific display-format detail found. Not found.

### 2. CPC, ACoS and ROAS benchmarks in India by category

- **CPC (Sponsored Products), India, all of these vendor figures:**
  - Platform average about Rs 8-25 per click, not broken out by category. [C, swcybernetics, 2026]
  - Broad categories Rs 8-35; premium or competitive niches (supplements, beauty, baby care) Rs 40-120+. [C, agency, 2026]
  - Platform average Rs 8-12; electronics Rs 15-35; fashion Rs 5-12. [C, 2026]
  - Electronics Rs 8-15 average, Rs 20-35 when competitive. [C, 2026]
  - Conflict: the four ranges disagree by a factor of 2-3x in the same category. No Amazon-published CPC table was found. Not found (official).
- **ACoS, India:**
  - Average across categories 25-35%. [C, OwlClaw benchmark page, 2025]
  - Target 15-25% for most categories; well-managed accounts 16-22% (vendor's own internal data, not audited). [C, swcybernetics, 2025-2026]
  - Advice: set target ACoS 5-15 points below gross margin. [C, OwlClaw; eVanik says no universal good ACoS]
- **ROAS:** 3:1-4:1 "good" in 2025 for $1M+ US sellers; another page says above 4:1. These are US figures, not India. [C, Titan Network, 2025-2026]
- **Category-level India ACoS/ROAS:** Not found.

### 3. How ad rank is decided, and how ads feed organic rank

- **Amazon's stated principle:** ranking score for each eligible ad from relevance and bid. Relevance has increasingly outweighed bid. About 92% of selected Sponsored Products ads in 2024 were not the highest bid. Advertisers name a max CPC; the price paid is set by the auction. [A/B as quoted from Amazon's own statement via ppc.land, 2024-2025; US auction]
- **Pricing claim:** one vendor says you pay about $0.01 above the second-highest bid. Unverified. [C]
- **Relevance inputs (vendor interpretation):** CTR history, conversion rate, listing quality (title, bullets). Ads failing a relevance threshold are dropped before ranking (feedvisor, 2025). [C, one-off claims, not confirmed by Amazon]
- **Ads feeding organic rank:** Commonly described as sales velocity and conversion from paid clicks increasing organic ranking for the same keyword. [C, several agency blogs; this is an agreed pattern across vendors but not stated by Amazon in anything I read]
- **India-specific ranking weights:** Not found.

### 4. Auto vs manual campaigns for a launch

- Vendor consensus (C): start with an automatic campaign to harvest search terms, then move winning terms to manual keyword campaigns with exact match and tune bids. Manual product targeting for competitor ASINs. Not found: India-specific data on which works better.

### 5. Amazon's AI ad tools in India, and AMC access

- **AI image generator:** beta in India late 2024; lifestyle images from the Ad Console. [B, exchange4media / afaqs, 2024]
- **AI video generator:** India, Nov 2025, through Creative Studio; free; builds video from product images, videos or detail pages; editable headlines and logo placement. [B; Amazon India launch note]
- **Creative Agent** (builds video and display ads from natural-language prompts) and **Ads Agent** (plans, launches and optimises campaigns; in AMC now, reaching Campaign Manager later in 2026): announced at Connected Worlds India, March 2026. [B, exchange4media / manifest-media / mediabrief, Mar 2026]
- **AMC:** Amazon India launch note dated 18 Sep 2025 says AMC is available to all advertisers running sponsored ads campaigns (Sponsored Products, Sponsored Display, Sponsored Brands, Sponsored TV), with Asia-Pacific listing India. [A, advertising.amazon.com hi-in and en, 2025-09-18]
- **AMC cost and minimum:** Not confirmed by Amazon. A vendor blog says free, billed through ad account, and cites an unofficial Rs 10 lakh/month DSP guideline. [C, unverified]
- **Older restriction:** Amazon Marketing Stream excluded India in 2022. Different product, older. [A, Amazon ad page; date uncertain]

### 6. Great Indian Festival 2025 and 2026

**GIF 2025 (reported Oct 2025):**
- 276 crore customer visits (about 2.76 billion). [A/B; search snippets citing Amazon press]
- Highest SMB seller participation in the first 48 hours; over two-thirds of these SMB sellers from tier 2 and 3 cities. [A/B, Amazon press via search snippet, Oct 2025]
- Highest-ever number of local shop sellers crossed Rs 10 lakh in sales. [A/B, same]
- Amazon Bazaar: sellers crossing Rs 1 lakh rose 15x year on year. [A/B, same]
- Rs 1,000 crore customer savings (fonearena headline; not verified). [B, unverified]
- 30,000 new product launches (fonearena headline; not verified). [B]
- **Sellers total count and GMV from Amazon: not found.**
- **Market-level GMV:** Rs 25,000 crore in first two days of online festive sales, +25% YoY; the source does not say whether Amazon-only. [B, Deccan Chronicle, 2025]
- **Sector festive GMV 2025:** more than Rs 1.15 lakh crore (Redseer, cited by a secondary summary). [B-secondary, 2025; sector, not Amazon]
- **Top categories:** Not found from Amazon. Only the 2026 coverage names smartphones, TVs, ACs and electronics as deal categories, not sales ranking.

**GIF 2026 (reported 2026):**
- Start date: 8 Oct 2026, with Prime members getting early access from 7 Oct. Several outlets agree. [B, mysmartprice, outlookbusiness, 91mobiles, fonearena; reported Sep-Oct 2026]
- Bank offer: 10% SBI Card discount; up to 10% extra for Prime members. [B; reported]
- Up to 75% off with 10% cashback in some listings. [B/C]
- End date: not confirmed in the results I read.
- Amazon quote: a VP at Amazon India said sellers' promotions give significant savings. Seller programmes for 2026: not found.
- **Conflict:** a few older pages (Zoutons, Analytics Insight) still say the date was unannounced. These look older.

### 7. Prime Day India 2025 and 2026

- **2025:** 12-14 July 2025, first three-day edition in India, 72 hours from 00:00 12 July to 23:59 14 July. Prime-members-focused, 400+ brands, thousands of launches, 17 Prime Video premieres (Amazon India page as quoted by search snippets). [A/B, June 2025]
- **2026:** reported as 4-6 July 2026 (10th Prime Day in India), with Prime Shopping Days from 7 July. [C/B aggregator reports, 2026]
  - Eligibility conflict: some listings say Prime-only, others say anyone could shop with early access for Prime members. One deal site says the follow-on sale was open to non-Prime users. Not resolved.
  - Bank offer: SBI and Axis Bank cards, up to 10% (one aggregator). [C, single source]
- **Prime Day 2026 is in the past** (today 30 Oct 2026). For planning, 2027 dates are not announced.

### 8. Lightning deals, coupons, Prime exclusive deals, eligibility and fees

- **US deal fees (not India):** from 2 June 2025 a daily fee plus a share of deal sales (Lightning: $70/day + 1.0% of deal sales, capped $2,000); Prime Day flat fees $1,000 (Best Deals) and $500 (Lightning, 2025). [C/seller forum, supplypike; 2025]
- **India deal fees:** official schedule not found. An India blog gives Rs 1,000-5,000 per Lightning Deal with 45-day lead time. [C, unverified]
- **Coupons (US fee):** $5 flat per coupon plus 2.5% of coupon-attributed sales, from June 2025. [C, sellerlabs-type guide; US dollars, India not confirmed]
- **Coupon eligibility:** Professional plan, seller rating 3.5 or above (one consultancy guide; India not confirmed). [C]
- **Lightning deal eligibility (India):** 5+ reviews and 3.5-star minimum (one India consultancy; not Amazon). [C, unverified]
- **Prime-exclusive deals (India):** official policy not found. The Prime Day structure above is the best reference.
- Deal eligibility in general: Amazon's deals dashboard shows eligible ASINs in Seller Central based on sales history, ratings and category. [C; not verified against Amazon India docs]

### 9. External traffic: Brand Referral Bonus in India

- BRB is described as US-only, requiring brand registration and Amazon Attribution tracking; only external traffic counts. [C, several guides; one French Amazon Ads post lists US brand owners as eligible]
- Hindi Amazon Ads page on the programme exists, but I could not confirm India eligibility from the snippet.
- **Verdict:** not confirmed live for Amazon.in sellers. Check Brand Registry and Amazon Ads for an enrolment option. [unresolved]

### 10. Influencer programme in India

- **Amazon Influencer Programme India:** launched 2018. About 2 lakh creators by Oct 2026 (report dated 19 Oct 2026, doubled YoY). Accepts creators with about 2,000 followers and engagement thresholds. No application fee. [B, storyboard18 / exchange4media / Amazon India, 2026]
- **Creator Connections** (Sept 2026): links creators to brand campaigns; reported 1.65 lakh creators at launch. [B, 8 Sep 2026; counts vary by source]
- **Tech Influencer Programme:** up to 20 days early access to launches, studio access, brand deals; above 1 lakh creators. [B, 2025-2026]
- **Elevate:** performance pilot, India-only, invite-only. [B, storyboard18]
- **Creator Central:** platform for creators in the programme. [B, inc42]
- **Creator demographics:** nearly 75% of Amazon influencers in India from non-metro cities (exchange4media headline; not verified). [B, unverified]

## Ranking / visibility mechanics

- **Paid rank (Sponsored Products):** score = relevance (vendor-described: CTR and conversion history, listing quality) x bid. Ads below a relevance threshold are dropped. Amazon says relevance has become more important than bid. [A/B, Amazon statement via ppc.land; vendor detail C]
- **Organic rank:** not directly published. Common view across vendors: sales velocity and conversion from paid and deal traffic feed organic ranking for the same search terms. [C, agreed pattern across several vendors, not Amazon]
- **Deals and events:** Prime Day and GIF put products on deal pages and bring Prime shoppers. Eligibility depends on sales history and ratings (C).
- **Influencer and external traffic:** AMC and Attribution data let brands see sales from external sources. BRB (if available) would pay for them; not confirmed for India.

## What a small new D2C seller should do / avoid (pattern, labelled)

**PATTERN (my inference from the facts above, not a sourced rule):**

- **Do:** start with auto campaigns to find search terms, then move winners to manual exact-match keywords. [C consensus]
- **Do:** set ACoS target from gross margin (target 5-15 points below margin). [C]
- **Do:** use Amazon's free AI image and video generators in Creative Studio before paying for outside creative. [B, free per coverage; check the current price in Ads console]
- **Do:** check AMC access in Ads console (official: available to sponsored-ads advertisers from Sep 2025). [A]
- **Do:** plan for Prime Day or GIF with deal fees in mind. For India, get the fee schedule from Seller Central before committing; none was verified here. [C]
- **Do:** watch the referral-fee changes (zero under Rs 300 from Apr 2025; reported Rs 1,000 threshold from Mar 2026). [A/B, check current schedule]
- **Avoid:** buying CPC or ACoS benchmarks from vendor tables as if they were official. Use your own first 30-60 days of data. [C]
- **Avoid:** assuming BRB works for Amazon.in. Not confirmed. [C]
- **Avoid:** planning around a Lightning Deal price from one blog. Get the fee in Seller Central. [C, unverified]
