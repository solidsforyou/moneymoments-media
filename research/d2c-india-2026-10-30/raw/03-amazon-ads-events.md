## Findings by sub-question

### 2. CPC, ACoS and ROAS benchmarks in India by category

- **CPC (Sponsored Products), India, vendor figures only:**
  - Platform average about Rs 8-25 per click, not broken out by category. [C, swcybernetics, 2026]
  - Broad categories Rs 8-35; premium or competitive niches (supplements, beauty, baby care) Rs 40-120+. [C, agency, 2026]
  - Platform average Rs 8-12; electronics Rs 15-35; fashion Rs 5-12. [C, 2026]
  - Electronics Rs 8-15 average, Rs 20-35 when competitive. [C, 2026]
  - Conflict: the four ranges disagree by 2-3x for the same category. No Amazon-published or independent CPC table found. Not found (official). Still unverified.
- **ACoS, India (vendor only):**
  - Average across categories 25-35%. [C, OwlClaw benchmark, 2025]
  - Target 15-25% for most categories; well-managed accounts 16-22% (vendor's internal data, not audited). [C, swcybernetics, 2025-2026]
  - Advice: set target ACoS 5-15 points below gross margin. [C, OwlClaw; eVanik says no universal good ACoS]
- **ROAS:** 3:1-4:1 "good" for $1M+ US sellers (Titan Network). US figures, not India. [C]
- **Category-level India ACoS/ROAS:** Not found.

### 3. How ad rank is decided, and how ads feed organic rank

- **Amazon's stated principle (Search summary, page not fetched):** Amazon applies a ranking score to each eligible ad from relevance and bid. Relevance has increasingly outweighed bid over time. About 92% of selected Sponsored Products ads in 2024 were not the highest bid. Cited to Amazon's FTC lawsuit response on aboutamazon.com (date not confirmed; US auction context). **Corrected:** raw file cited ppc.land as the source; the search result points to Amazon's own page, which is the better primary source. Still needs a fetch.
- **Relevance threshold:** an agency source says ads must meet minimum relevance criteria to enter the auction. [C, m19.com, secondary]
- **Pricing claim:** one vendor says you pay about $0.01 above the second-highest bid. Unverified. [C]
- **Relevance inputs (vendor interpretation):** CTR history, conversion rate, listing quality. [C, one-off claims, not confirmed by Amazon]
- **Ads feeding organic rank:** agreed pattern across agency blogs (sales velocity and conversion from paid clicks help organic rank for the same keyword). Not stated by Amazon in anything read. [C]
- **Amazon Ads Hindi "ad relevance" page (source 40): corrected.** A search summary says this page covers Amazon DSP, not Sponsored Products. Do not cite it for Sponsored Products relevance. Search summary, page not fetched.
- **India-specific ranking weights:** Not found.

### 4. Auto vs manual campaigns for a launch

- Vendor consensus [C]: start with an automatic campaign to harvest search terms, then move winning terms to manual keyword campaigns with exact match. Manual product targeting for competitor ASINs. No India-specific data on which works better. Not found.

## Ranking / visibility mechanics

- **Paid rank (Sponsored Products):** score = relevance x bid, with relevance weighted more heavily over time (Amazon statement per search summary; the exact formula is not published). Ads below a relevance threshold do not enter the auction. [A, search summary; vendor detail C]
- **Organic rank:** not directly published by Amazon. Common vendor view: sales velocity and conversion from paid and deal traffic feed organic rank for the same search terms. [C]
- **Deals and events:** Prime Day and GIF put products on deal pages and bring Prime shoppers. Eligibility depends on sales history and ratings. [C]
- **Influencer and external traffic:** AMC and Attribution data let brands see sales from external sources. BRB would pay for them; not confirmed for India.

## What a small new D2C seller should do / avoid (pattern, labelled)

**PATTERN (inference from the facts above, not a sourced rule):**

- **Do:** start with auto campaigns to find search terms, then move winners to manual exact-match keywords. [C consensus]
- **Do:** set ACoS target from gross margin (5-15 points below margin). [C]
- **Do:** check the free AI image and video tools in the Ads console before paying for outside creative. [B, free per coverage; unverified]
- **Do:** check AMC access in the Ads console. [A as listed, unverified]
- **Do:** plan for Prime Day or GIF with deal fees in mind. Get the India fee schedule from Seller Central before committing. None was verified. [C]
- **Do:** watch referral-fee changes (zero under Rs 300 from Apr 2025, reported zero under Rs 1,000 from Mar 2026). Check the current schedule on Seller Central. [A/B, unverified]
- **Avoid:** using vendor CPC or ACoS tables as if they were official. Use your own first 30-60 days of data. [C]
- **Avoid:** assuming BRB works for Amazon.in. Not confirmed; leaning not available. [C]
- **Avoid:** planning around a Lightning Deal price from one blog. Get the fee in Seller Central. [C, unverified]
