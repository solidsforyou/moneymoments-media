# 10. Google, YouTube and AI-driven discovery for Indian e-commerce (2025-2026)

Prepared 30 Oct 2026 for SOLIDS FOR YOU (Amazon.in seller, small D2C brand).

## Key takeaways

1. Google AI Overviews reached India in August 2024, with English and Hindi support (Business Today, 16 Aug 2024, snippet). Hindi AI Mode arrived in September 2025 and Search Live in English and Hindi in October 2025 (MediaNama Sep 2025 and TechCrunch 8 Oct 2025, snippet only). The Labs gate on AI Mode in India was removed a few weeks after its June 2025 launch (search summary; primary blog not opened).
2. Organic clicks fall where AI summaries appear. Published studies put the cut at roughly 34% (Ahrefs, Apr 2025) to about 61% (Seer, Nov 2025) for organic CTR. All of these are US-centric and not India-measured. Treat the range as a warning, not a forecast.
3. Google has offered free product listings through Merchant Center, and India is on the eligible-country list (Google help page, snippet only). The listings can appear in the Shopping tab, Google Images, Lens and YouTube. Recent reports say sponsored tiles now sit inside the free-listing grid (PPC Land, Apr 2026, C). Free listings are a no-cost channel, but they are not a guaranteed placement.
4. No India-specific 2025 CPC benchmark for Shopping or Performance Max was found. Agency blogs give ranges from about Rs 5-15 to Rs 15-80 per click, and they conflict (C). A reasonable planning band is Rs 10-50 per click, which is my synthesis, not a sourced figure.
5. Performance Max needs about 30+ conversions in 30 days to leave its learning phase, according to an agency blog (C). That implies a budget floor for a new brand. The Rs 50,000-1,50,000 per month figure is also agency-sourced and unverified.
6. YouTube Shopping affiliate programme launched in India in October 2024 with Flipkart and Myntra (YouTube/Google blog and Business Standard, Oct 2024). Nykaa, Purplle, Shoppers Stop and Tira followed in 2025. A later expansion adds Amazon, AJIO, Meesho, Snapdeal and Tata CLiQ; its exact date is not confirmed (a summary says Sept 2026).
7. YouTube reports that over 50% of eligible Indian creators are enrolled in the affiliate programme as of May 2026, and that creator payouts rose more than 160% from Q1 2025 to Q1 2026 (self-reported, B/A). Treat both as YouTube's own numbers.
8. Google's 2025 India Year in Search lists IPL as the top search, Gemini as the top AI search, and Labubu as the top collectible. It has no retail or brand shopping list in the sources found (snippet only). The official Year in Search page was not opened.
9. Amazon Rufus has been live in India since August-September 2024 (Business Standard, 20 Sep 2024). ChatGPT added a shopping route for Indian D2C brands in July 2026 through GoKwik and PayU, with checkout and UPI (secondary press, snippet). Flipkart's first assistant, Flippi, launched October 2023. Flipkart's replacement, SLAP, was reported in January 2026 and is unconfirmed as launched.
10. For AI-search visibility, the consistent (but C-rated) advice is: a complete merchant feed, Product and Organization schema, reviews, and off-site mentions in comparison content. No independent India case study with measured citation lift was found.
11. Vernacular and voice search share for India in 2025 was not found. The best available numbers are from 2016-2019 Google/KPMG work (for example, 234 million Indian-language internet users versus 175 million English users in 2016). Those are old and should not be used as current facts.

## Ranking / visibility mechanics

Labelled FACT and PATTERN.

- FACT (sourced, US data): clicks to organic results drop on queries where AI Overviews appear (Ahrefs, Seer, Pew studies above). The India-specific placement of AI Overviews and AI Mode results was not verified in this run.
- FACT (sourced): Shopping and free listings require an approved Merchant Center feed. Only approved products appear in the Shopping tab.
- FACT (sourced, company-reported): YouTube Shopping tags are the path from video to purchase on Flipkart, Myntra, Nykaa, Purplle and (from 2026) Amazon.
- PATTERN (inference): Product data quality (feed, schema, reviews) is the common input for Google Shopping, ChatGPT shopping and Rufus. Improving it helps across all three at once.
- PATTERN (inference): Paid Shopping and PMax results depend on conversion volume; a small brand may need enough budget to exit learning, or it may get more from free listings and organic/AI visibility first.
- Unverified: Amazon restricts external AI crawlers. Do not plan around it until confirmed.

## What a small new D2C seller should do / avoid (pattern, labelled)

PATTERN (my inference, not a sourced rule):

- Do:
  - Turn on free Merchant Center listings for India and keep the feed clean (titles, GTIN or MPN, price, stock, images).
  - Add Product and Organization schema and mark up reviews on the brand site. Cheap, and it helps every AI surface that reads the page.
  - Build a short set of YouTube Shorts with product tags only after the feed and site are clean. Track affiliate tags through YouTube Shopping partner merchants.
  - Test Shopping or PMax on a small budget, and measure cost per click against your own margins before scaling.
  - Write pages that answer "best X for Y" and "X vs Y" in plain language, with citations to reviews. These get used in AI answers (C-rated).
- Avoid:
  - Relying on Google CTR from AI-Overview-covered informational queries. Plan for lower organic clicks on those.
  - Trusting agency CPC ranges as budget inputs. Use your own account data.
  - Adding /llms.txt or similar tricks as a main strategy without evidence.
  - Assuming Rufus, ChatGPT or Flipkart Flippi will show your listing without a clean feed and reviews.

- AI Mode India: the June 2025 Labs launch is from a search summary; the primary Google blog was not opened.
- CPC: Rs 5-15 (agency A) vs Rs 15-80 (agency B) vs Rs 8-40 (agency C). No authoritative India figure.
- Free listings and sponsored tiles: PPC Land reports sponsored tiles inside the free-listing grid (Apr 2026). Unconfirmed for India.
- Amazon blocking external AI crawlers: single unconfirmed source.
- Flipkart SLAP and Flippi: whether SLAP launched is unconfirmed (Jan 2026 report).
- Google-Flipkart AI shopping test (Sep 2026): URL-only, unverified.
- YouTube "over 50% of eligible creators" and "payouts up 160%": YouTube self-reported.
- ChatGPT India shopping (GoKwik/PayU, Jul 2026) vs OpenAI-Razorpay pilot (Oct 2025): link between them not confirmed.
- Eligibility of 10,000 subscribers for YouTube affiliate: repost, unverified.
- Gaps still open: India CPC benchmark from Google; voice and vernacular 2025 share; Hindi/Hinglish SEO data; Google Year in Search shopping list; Merchant Center India 2025 policy changes.
