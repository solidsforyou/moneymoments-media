# 11. Indian online shopper and macro picture, 2025-2026

Prepared 30 Oct 2026 for the India D2C sweep (SOLIDS FOR YOU, Amazon.in seller prep).

**Coverage note.** Direct fetches of bain.com, pib.gov.in and exchange4media.com failed (host did not resolve in this session), and one Redseer search hit a rate limit. Everything below rests on search-result snippets and secondary press coverage, so treat figures as "reported by" rather than verified against the primary PDF. Primary documents to check before quoting: Bain "How India Shops Online 2025/2026", PIB GST factsheet, PIB Budget 2026 key takeaways, RBI press releases, NPCI monthly UPI data.

## Key takeaways

1. **Market size (reported):** Bain's "How India Shops Online 2026" puts 2025 e-retail GMV at about $65-66 bn, up 19-21% y/y, with a shopper base of 290-300 mn in 2025 [B, via Brandinginasia; Bain 2026 edition]. This is well below the $160 bn-by-2028 trajectory Bain projected in its 2025 edition (about $60 bn in 2023 doubling to $160 bn by 2028), so the older forecast looks optimistic [B, Inside Retail, 8 Apr 2025].
2. **Latest Redseer reading:** Redseer puts H1 2026 e-commerce GMV at about $38 bn, up roughly 25% y/y, its strongest first half in four years. It expects grocery to account for nearly a third of added GMV [B, Redseer via search snippet, 2026; date of exact release not confirmed].
3. **Festive 2025 (estimate, not platform-reported):** Redseer estimated festive-month e-commerce GMV at about Rs 1.15 lakh crore (about $13 bn), up 20-25% y/y and its strongest festive period in five years [B, Redseer, forecast issued ahead of Diwali 2025]. Amazon and Flipkart have not published their own GMV totals; all festive numbers are third-party estimates.
4. **Festive 2026 has started strongly, but only early, third-party data exists:** Redseer estimates about Rs 24,000 crore of GMV in the first two days of festive sales, with 8-9 crore orders [B, YourStory, Oct 2026]. A separate Redseer read puts the opening at over $2.5 bn with 6-6.5 crore shoppers, about two-thirds from tier-2 cities and rural areas [B, Redseer via search snippet, Oct 2026]. Amazon's India VP said early trends "beat our most optimistic expectations" [A/B, company quote via YourStory, Oct 2026]. No post-Diwali totals were found.
5. **Diwali 2026 timing:** Diwali is 8 Nov 2026, about three weeks later than in 2025. Amazon's sale opened 8 Oct 2026 and Flipkart's on 9 Oct, with early access for Plus/Black/card members on 8 Oct [B, press; dates as reported]. Expect the big-spend window to run through early November.
6. **GST 2.0 (effective 22 Sep 2025):** Four rates were consolidated into 5% and 18% slabs plus 40% on select goods. Apparel priced up to Rs 2,500 per piece moved to 5% (from Rs 1,000 threshold). [A-claimed via PIB factsheet, quoted in search snippet; not fetched directly.] Pre-reform FMCG forecasts (CLSA: 6-11% price cuts) were not borne out in clean data; early reads are mixed (NielsenIQ: FMCG growth slowed to 5.4% in Q2 amid the transition) [B].
7. **Apparel is split by price point:** Crisil says the 18% rate above Rs 2,500 could temper premium apparel growth, but it also projected organised apparel revenue growth lifted by about 200 bps, to about 14% (pre-implementation forecast) [B, Crisil via Free Press Journal]. CMAI expected apparel costs to rise 8-10% over the festive season, with consumer prices up 5-7% as brands absorbed part of it [B].
8. **Inflation fell sharply after the cut, then drifted up:** Headline CPI hit 1.54% in Sep 2025 (99-month low) [B, ETV Bharat/AsiaNews]; Oct 2025 CPI was about 0.25% per NSO, with analysts attributing about 85 bps of the drop to GST cuts [B, Outlook Business; NSO figure single-sourced]. A June 2026 rise to 4.4% is reported by a single aggregator, attributed to food and fuel [C-level; unverified].
9. **RBI:** Repo rate was cut 25 bps to 5.25% in Dec 2025 and held at 5.25% in Feb, Apr and Aug 2026 meetings [B/C, aggregator reports; the August hold is reported by several sites]. Conflicting snippets exist about June 2026 forecasts. Verify against RBI press releases.
10. **UPI is at record volumes but growth is slowing:** Monthly volumes in 2026 reached 24.51 bn in Aug (record), then 24.07 bn in Sep (value Rs 29.37 lakh crore). FY26 total was about 218.6 bn transactions and Rs 284.7 lakh crore [B, NPCI data via Entrackr, StartupTalky, Deccan Chronicle, Angel One]. One source puts Sep y/y growth at 23%, down from 31% a year earlier.
11. **Budget 2025 income-tax relief:** Zero tax on net taxable income up to Rs 12 lakh (Rs 12.75 lakh for salaried after the Rs 75,000 standard deduction), up from Rs 7 lakh; about 1 crore taxpayers affected [B, Business Standard/Business Today, 1-2 Feb 2025]. Consumption effect is forecast only; no measured spending impact was found.
12. **Budget 2026 e-commerce exports:** The Rs 10 lakh per-consignment cap on courier exports was removed, which helps small cross-border sellers. CBIC reportedly operationalised it afterwards [B/C, exchange4media and trade blogs; date of CBIC notice not confirmed].
13. **Tier-2+ is the growth engine:** Redseer's 2026 festive opening attributes about two-thirds of shoppers to tier-2 and rural areas [B, search snippet]. Older Redseer work (2022 forecast) put 57% of festive shoppers in tier-2+ [B, dated 2022; flag as old]. No clean 2025 order-share split was found.
14. **Gen Z is a discovery-driven, premium-curious cohort:** Gen Z shoppers are reported as 40-45% of India's e-retail base and nearly half of incremental orders in 2025 [B, Bain via Outlook Business; date ~2025]. About 70% discover brands online and buy faster (Bain 2025) [B]. More than seven in ten Gen Z buy premium/luxury brands at least sometimes, but "only premium" buying is limited to the top income bracket [B, Business Today, Jan 2026]. 89% of Indian Gen Z are willing to pay extra (Deloitte 2025) [B, date ~2025; snippet truncated].
15. **Social commerce is real but its size is unclear:** Grips Intelligence (Aug 2025-Apr 2026 data) puts Facebook at about 84% of social traffic to e-commerce sites, Instagram 8.3%, WhatsApp 5.2%, and WhatsApp converts best (5.5% vs 2.5% for Instagram) [C, vendor data]. Market-size numbers (e.g., "$8.42 bn social commerce in 2025") are from a research-firm databook with no visible method [C]. Older Bain/Sequoia and RedSeer forecasts (2020-21) projected much more and look overstated [B, dated].

### 4. GST 2.0 (rate rationalisation, Sep 2025) and effect on prices/demand

- **FACT:** GST rates moved from four slabs to two (5% and 18%) plus 40% on select goods; effective 22 Sep 2025. [A-claimed via PIB factsheet, quoted in search snippet; https://pib.gov.in/FactsheetDetails.aspx?Id=150293 (not fetched; host unreachable); date 22 Sep 2025]
- **FACT:** Apparel priced up to Rs 2,500 per piece moved to 5% (from the earlier Rs 1,000 threshold). [A-claimed via PIB, as above]
- **FACT:** Pre-implementation forecasts: CLSA estimated 6-11% FMCG price cuts with meaningful demand lift; another estimate 8-10% retail price reduction. [B; Outlook Business, Sep 2025; https://www.outlookbusiness.com/markets/fmcg-stocks-surge-as-gst-20-promises-demand-boost-britannia-colgate-dabur-soars-3-6]
- **FACT:** NielsenIQ reported FMCG sales growth slowed to 5.4% in Q2 2025 amid the GST transition. [B; date ~Q3 2025; source via search snippet]
- **FACT:** About 20 of 36 states/UTs saw GST revenue fall in Oct 2025. [B; Business Standard]. Centre projected net revenue loss of about Rs 47,000 crore. [B; same period]
- **FACT:** Crisil: 18% rate above Rs 2,500 could temper growth for premium apparel retailers; organised apparel revenue growth lifted about 200 bps to about 14% [B; Crisil via Free Press Journal, Sep 2025; https://www.freepressjournal.in/business/gst-rationalisation-set-to-lift-revenue-growth-of-apparel-retailers-by-200-bps-at-14]
- **FACT:** CMAI said reforms had not moved prices much; apparel costs up 8-10% over festive season, consumer prices up 5-7% (brands absorbing part). [B; Statesman/CMAI; Sep-Oct 2025]
- **FACT:** CPI fell to 1.54% in Sep 2025 (from 2.07% in Aug), with durables and autos down 0.2-2.0% m/m. [B; ETV Bharat / AsiaNews; Oct 2025; https://asianews.network/indias-headline-inflation-for-september-eases-to-99-month-low-at-1-54/]
- **FACT:** October 2025 CPI about 0.25% (NSO), with food at -5.02%; analysts attributed about 85 bps of the drop to GST cuts. [B/C; Outlook Business; single-source NSO figure; https://www.outlookbusiness.com/economy-and-policy/gst-rate-cuts-pull-down-oct-inflation-by-85-bps-declining-trend-to-continue-experts]
- **FACT:** September 2026 CPI print was **not found**.
- **PATTERN:** The price effect was largest where the rate fell most (small-ticket apparel, FMCG, consumer durables); premium apparel above Rs 2,500 faces the 18% rate, so price points just above the threshold are a pricing decision, not a tax windfall.

- **PATTERN/forecast:** Analysts (FMCG, autos, durables) expected a demand lift. No measured consumption outcome was found in this sweep. [forecast, B]

### 9. Gen Z shopping behaviour

- **FACT:** 70% of Gen Z shoppers discover brands online and make quicker purchases, browsing fewer listings than millennials. [B; Bain 2025]

### 10. Value vs premium split ("premiumisation" vs "Bharat value")

- **FACT:** Premium buying is common but mostly occasional for Gen Z; exclusivity is limited to top income (see 9). [B]
- **FACT:** Quality and price are the top drivers, not trends (one study). [C]
- **PATTERN:** The split is by occasion and income, not by person. A large mass of shoppers buy value most days and premium on specific occasions (festive, gifting). Festive-season tier-2 growth (see 2) supports this.
- **Not found:** A clean 2025 value-vs-premium GMV split from Redseer or Bain.

## Ranking / visibility mechanics

- Mostly not applicable to macro data. Relevant pattern: festive-season GMV is concentrated in the first two to three days of each sale (Early Access), and platforms publish their own "record" claims at the start. Expect sale-day spikes and headline inflation in the first week.
- **PATTERN (C-rated, no source found in this sweep):** Algorithm claims (e.g., how Amazon.in ranks listings during sales) were not researched here; that belongs to a different sub-topic.

## What a small new D2C seller should do / avoid (pattern, labelled)

- **PATTERN:** Plan the festive push around the early-access window (8-9 Oct 2026) and the Diwali peak (8 Nov 2026). Platform data shows the first days carry much of the GMV.
- **PATTERN:** Price-test apparel and FMCG around the GST thresholds. Under GST 2.0, price points just above Rs 2,500 (apparel) carry 18% tax; do not assume a lower price point is always the winner.
- **PATTERN:** For tier-2/3 demand, prioritise delivery speed, COD options and regional-language listings. Tier-2+ share is rising in every source; quality of evidence for specific tactics is weak.
- **PATTERN:** Gen Z discovery is social-first; plan Instagram/WhatsApp content alongside marketplace listings. WhatsApp converts better than Instagram in the one dataset found (C-level).
- **PATTERN:** Do not build a forecast on the 2020-21 social-commerce or e-retail projections; they are dated and look overstated.
- **PATTERN:** Budget 2026 courier-cap removal helps cross-border sales; check current CBIC notice before relying on it.
- **AVOID:** Using Redseer/Bain numbers without the year and the release date. Several figures here are forecasts presented as results.
