4. **Corrected: Bain's 2025 forecast is $170-190 bn by 2030, not "$160 bn by 2028".** The Bain 2025 page says e-retail to reach an estimated $170-190 bn GMV by 2030, growing over 18% a year. ## What a small new D2C seller should do / avoid (pattern, labelled)

- **PATTERN:** Festive 2026 is a $15-16 bn season (Redseer projection). Quick commerce grows about 110-120% in it, and mobiles and electronics fall below half of GMV. Plan category and channel mix for that, not for last year's mobile-led season.
- **PATTERN:** Festive early access (8-9 Oct 2026) has passed. The Diwali peak (8 Nov 2026) is as reported, not checked against an official calendar.
- **PATTERN:** Price-test apparel and footwear around the Rs 2,500 threshold. Above it the rate is 18%.
- **PATTERN:** Rates are rising. The RBI hiked to 5.50% and CPI is 5.54% (Sep 2026). Expect tighter consumer cash and higher finance costs for working capital.
- **PATTERN:** Tier 2+ is about half of incremental orders (Bain) and about two-thirds of festive shoppers (Redseer). Prioritise delivery speed, COD options and regional-language listings. Tactic-level evidence is still weak.
- **PATTERN:** Gen Z is discovery-led and growing faster in GMV share than in users. Plan Instagram and WhatsApp content alongside marketplace listings. WhatsApp converting better than Instagram rests on one vendor dataset.
- **PATTERN:** Do not build a forecast on 2020-21 social-commerce projections or the press "$160 bn by 2028" figure. Use Bain's own 2030 range.
- **PATTERN:** The courier export cap is gone from 1 Apr 2026, which helps cross-border sellers. Check the CBIC circular before relying on it.
- **AVOID:** Quoting Redseer or Bain figures without the year and whether they are forecasts or outcomes.
