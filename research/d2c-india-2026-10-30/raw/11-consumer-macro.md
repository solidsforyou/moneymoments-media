Caution: the series is now base 2024=100, so y/y comparisons with the 2025 prints in this file are not like-for-like. [A, MoSPI release, via search; PDF not fetched]
3. **Market size (Verified in pass 2, secondary only):** Bain "How India Shops Online 2026" puts 2025 e-retail GMV at about $65-66 bn (+19-21% y/y), with 290-300 mn shoppers. Gen Z is 40-45% of e-retail shoppers and drove about half of incremental orders in 2025. Coverage describes the report as prepared with Flipkart, so treat it as partly platform-linked. [B]
4. **Bain's older forecast is behind the actual path.** The 2025 edition's "$160 bn by 2028" figure is confirmed in press. Bain's 2026 edition projects $170-180 bn by 2030 at over 20% a year. One snippet quotes the 2025 edition as $170-190 bn by 2030, which conflicts with the 2028 figure. [B, conflicting]
5. **Redseer H1 2026 (secondary only):** e-commerce GMV about $38 bn, up about 25% y/y, the strongest first half in four years. Quick commerce is 22% of online retail (up from 14%), about $9 bn in H1. Redseer forecasts 2026 at over $90 bn (+22-24%). Grocery's share of online retail GMV rises from 16% (2025) to about 19% (2026). [B]
6. **Festive 2026 opening (Redseer, reported 11 Oct 2026):** about Rs 24,000 crore (over $2.5 bn) in the first two days, with about 60-65 mn shoppers. Two-thirds came from tier-2 and rural areas. Fashion GMV grew 22-25% y/y and made 15-18% of the total. Gen Z made up about a third of opening-day buyers. [B, Redseer via press; not a platform disclosure]
7. **Corrected: festive 2025 total of Rs 1.15 lakh crore is a forecast, not a Redseer outcome.** Redseer's own first-leg read was Rs 60-62k crore (+20-22%). Quote the Rs 1.15 lakh crore only as "Redseer forecast", never as the final figure. [B]
8. **GST 2.0 (Verified in pass 2, secondary only):** since 22 Sep 2025, apparel and footwear priced up to Rs 2,500 are at 5%, and above Rs 2,500 at 18%. Before, the 5% band stopped at Rs 1,000 and the rate was 12% above that. The PIB factsheet was not reached. [A-claimed, trade press consistent]
9. **Corrected: the Budget 2026 courier export cap is confirmed.** The Rs 10 lakh per-consignment cap on courier-mode exports was removed from 1 Apr 2026 (Customs Notifications 33 and 34/2026 (NT), both dated 31 Mar 2026; DGFT Notification 67/2025-26 dated 27 Mar 2026). Circular number is inconsistent across sources (1/2026 vs 17/2026). [A-claimed, via trade and tax press]
10. **Budget 2026 MSME (secondary only):** Rs 10,000 crore SME Growth Fund; CGTMSE credit guarantee extended to invoice discounting on TReDS; CPSE MSME payments routed through TReDS; GeM linked to TReDS. The Rs 25,060 crore export mission cited in one item is dated April 2026 and may not be a Budget measure. [B, not verified against the Budget document]
11. **UPI (NPCI data via press):** Aug 2026 24.51 bn transactions (Rs 29.82 lakh crore, record). Sep 2026 24.07 bn (Rs 29.37 lakh crore). Daily transactions crossed 800 mn for the first time in Sep. October 2026 data is not yet out (as at 30 Oct). [B]
12. **Tier-2+ is the growth engine, but there is no clean order-share number for 2025-26.** The two-thirds share is the Redseer 2026 festive shopper figure. The 57% tier-2+ figure is a 2022 Redseer forecast and is old. [B]
13. **Gen Z is a large, discovery-led cohort.** 40-45% of shoppers and about half of incremental orders (Bain 2026, via press). The "70% discover brands online" figure is still from Bain 2025, not re-checked. [B]

## What a small new D2C seller should do / avoid (pattern, labelled)

- **PATTERN:** Plan the festive push around the early-access window (8-9 Oct 2026, already past) and the Diwali peak (8 Nov 2026, date as reported; not checked against an official calendar). The 2026 opening was led by mobiles and fashion, with fashion +22-25% y/y.
- **PATTERN:** Price-test apparel and footwear around the Rs 2,500 threshold. Above it the rate is 18%. Set prices with the tax in mind; do not assume a lower price point is always the winner.
- **PATTERN (updated):** Rates are rising, not falling. The RBI hiked to 5.50% and CPI is 5.54%. Expect tighter cash for consumers and higher finance costs for working capital. Plan the cash cycle for that.
- **PATTERN:** Tier-2/rural is about two-thirds of festive shoppers (Redseer 2026). Prioritise delivery speed, COD options and regional-language listings. Quality of evidence for specific tactics is still weak.
- **PATTERN:** Gen Z is discovery-led and about a third of festive opening-day buyers. Plan Instagram and WhatsApp content alongside marketplace listings. WhatsApp converting better than Instagram rests on one vendor dataset (C-level).
- **PATTERN:** Do not build a forecast on the 2020-21 social-commerce projections or the 2028 e-retail trajectory. Bain's 2026 figures show it running behind.
- **PATTERN:** The courier export cap is removed from 1 Apr 2026, which helps cross-border sellers. Check the current CBIC circular before relying on it.
- **AVOID:** Quoting Redseer or Bain figures without the year and whether they are forecasts or outcomes.
