# 12 - Regulation: rules a D2C brand selling online in India must follow (as of 30 Oct 2026)

Research status: partial. Two WebFetch hosts (scconline.com, storyboard18.com) failed to resolve in this session, so no primary text was read. Several WebSearch calls were rate-limited (CCPA self-audit, IS 19000 status, initial dark-patterns query). Almost every claim below comes from search-result snippets and secondary coverage. Treat every date and rule as "verify against the gazette / regulator site before acting." Source ratings follow RULES.md (A = official, B = reputable press or filings, C = blogs, law-firm explainers, news aggregators).

## Key takeaways

1. The Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules were amended in 2026. Secondary reports say notified 9 Sept 2026, effective 1 Jan 2027, and contraventions on or after that date fall under the Consumer Protection Act 2019. Contents of the amendment were not verified. Treat as a must-check before go-live. [C, rating of news items]
2. Dark patterns are regulated under the CCPA (India's Central Consumer Protection Authority) Guidelines for Prevention and Regulation of Dark Patterns, 2023. The annex lists 13 prohibited practices, including false urgency, basket sneaking, subscription traps and drip pricing. [B, secondary summaries]
3. On 5 June 2025 CCPA issued an advisory under s.18(1) of the Consumer Protection Act 2019 telling all e-commerce platforms to self-audit their interfaces for dark patterns within three months and to submit self-declarations. Some reports call the declarations voluntary, so the status is unclear. [B, storyboard18 (date from snippet), not fetched]
4. Reported enforcement: DoCA reportedly issued notices to 11 e-commerce platforms under the 2023 Guidelines, and a joint working group was set up to identify violations. [B/C, single secondary source]
5. Fake reviews: BIS IS 19000:2022 ("Online Consumer Reviews - Principles and Requirements for their Collection, Moderation and Publication") came into effect 25 Nov 2022. Sources disagree on whether it is binding. One says voluntary, with mandatory status possible if fake reviews persist. No evidence found of a mandatory notification as of Oct 2026. [B, Business Today 13 Dec 2022; J.Sagar law firm newsletter]
6. Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Amendment Rules 2026 (notified 13 Feb 2026 per one source) add a country-of-origin rule for e-commerce. Platforms selling imported products must offer a searchable and sortable country-of-origin filter. Reported effective date 1 July 2026. Sources conflict on rule number (6(10) in the Oct 2025 draft, 6(10A) in the final per one source) and on a separate "Second Amendment Rules, 2025" dated 2 Dec 2025 (effective 1 Feb 2026, changing Rule 26(a)). Confirm the gazette. [C, SCC Online 21 Feb 2026 (not fetched); draft: Drishti 12 Nov 2025]
7. No verified change to MRP, unit sale price, net quantity or manufacturer-detail rules in the 2026 amendments was found in this search. The only unit-sale-price change found is the 2023 amendment exempting combination, group and multi-piece packs. Legal Metrology Packaged Commodities Rules 2011 remain the base text for listing content. [C, TeamLease 2023 amendment article]
8. QCOs: BIS withdrew QCOs on 14 products via a gazette dated 12 Nov 2025 (mostly polyester and petrochemical inputs). The Ministry of Textiles dropped the VSF QCO on 18 Nov 2025. The Cotton Bales QCO 2023 was rescinded by S.O. 2956(E), reportedly dated 9 June 2026. A Gujarat High Court order of 4 May 2026 stayed removal of QCOs on FDY and POY yarns. No confirmed QCO change for finished apparel found; one headline suggests the Footwear QCO 2020 was rescinded, but its date and status could not be confirmed. [B for Nov 2025 items (Business Today 18 Nov 2025; The Week 14 Nov 2025); C for the rest]
9. GST TCS under s.52 (CGST Act) is 0.5% of net taxable value (0.25% CGST + 0.25% SGST intra-state, or 0.5% IGST inter-state), effective 10 July 2024 under Notification 15/2024-CT. Multiple mid-2026 guides still show 0.5%; no 2026 change found. Some older guides still say 1%, which is stale. TCS is filed by the e-commerce operator in GSTR-8. [C; LKS May 2026 newsletter listed but not read]
10. GST registration: inter-state supply makes registration mandatory regardless of turnover. Sources disagree on whether the 2023 small-seller intra-state exemption for goods sellers through a platform still applies in full. Treat as open and confirm with a CA before onboarding. [C, conflicting]
11. DPDP Act 2023 and DPDP Rules 2025 were notified in November 2025 (13 or 14 Nov, depending on source). Consent-manager provisions apply after 12 months. Most substantive duties (notices, consent, breach notification, security safeguards, cross-border transfer) apply after 18 months, around 13-14 May 2027. Business Today reported talks to shorten the timeline; no adoption found. [B, Business Today 19 Nov 2025; EY Nov 2025 alert]
12. ASCI: the Influencer Advertising Guidelines (2021/2023 base) were updated in April 2025 (press release, primary). Secondary blogs claim a 2026 update adds AI virtual-influencer disclosure and a verbal disclosure in the first 10 seconds of video. Unverified; check ascionline.in before using. [A for Apr 2025 press release; C for 2026 claims]
13. FDI: Press Note 3 (2026 Series), reportedly dated 23 July 2026, lets FDI-backed marketplace entities run an inventory-based model only for exports. Domestic B2C inventory-model FDI remains barred. Sources disagree on whether it is in force (needs FEMA/NDI notification). [B, EY Sept 2026 alert; C for others]
14. CCI antitrust probe into Amazon and Flipkart: Supreme Court refused in 2021 to stop the preliminary inquiry. High courts stayed proceedings in 2024 (Karnataka HC interim stay, 27 Sept 2024 per one source). CCI asked the Supreme Court in late 2025 to hear 23 related petitions (Pakistan Today header Jan 2026, date unclear). No Supreme Court ruling on that request found. Status as of Oct 2026: unresolved, unconfirmed. [B/C, mixed dates]
15. Export hubs: Districts as Export Hubs (DEH) is a framework covering 770+ districts (Jan 2026 figure). Only 249 of 590 district export plans were formally adopted as of March 2026. E-Commerce Export Hubs (ECEH) are under Chapter 9 of the Foreign Trade Policy 2023; some hubs reportedly approved for pilot. DGFT runs platform MoUs (Amazon extended Aug 2025; Shiprocket, DHL, others in talks). [B/C; PIB doc dated 2 Sept 2026 listed but not read]

## Findings by sub-question

### 1. Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules 2020 and amendments
- Amended rules reported as notified 9 Sept 2026, effective 1 Jan 2027. Stated aim: strengthen consumer protection while keeping a transparent, balanced framework. [C, Manorama Yearbook 11 Sept 2026: https://www.manoramayearbook.in/current-affairs/india/2026/09/11/govt-amends-e-commerce-rules.html]
- Original 2020 Rules (July 2020) set duties and liabilities of platforms and sellers. [C]
- 2021 draft proposed defining cross-selling, fall-back liability and flash sales, and a resident nodal officer. Not confirmed whether these made it into the final 2026 text. [C, iPleaders / Mondaq 2021 commentary; date older than Jan 2025, flagged as possibly outdated]
- Gazette text not read. Action: pull the 2026 amendment from the DoCA site.

### 2. CCPA dark patterns (2023 guidelines, 2025-2026 enforcement)
- 2023 Guidelines list 13 practices (false urgency, basket sneaking, subscription traps, drip pricing, etc.). [B, storyboard18 summary: https://www.storyboard18.com/digital/ccpa-warns-e-comm-firms-over-dark-patterns-self-audit-within-3-months-69249.htm, June 2025]
- 5 June 2025 advisory: self-audit within 3 months; self-declaration of no dark patterns. [B; indialaw.in https://www.indialaw.in/blog/civil/ccpas-dark-patterns-mandates-self-audit/ C; FoxMandal https://foxmandal.in/news/dark-patterns-back-in-spotlight-as-new-advisory-calls-for-self-audits/ C]
- Criticism that enforceability and remedies are weak. [C, https://www.internetfreedom.in/dark-patterns-and-weak-remedies-why-the-ccpa-advisory-falls-short/, date not shown]
- Enforcement: notices to 11 platforms; joint working group. [B/C, single source, storyboard18]
- Not found: any 2026 penalty order or new 2026 guideline. Note: "CCPA" in India is the Central Consumer Protection Authority, not California's privacy agency. Searches returned California CCPA only as noise.

### 3. Fake review rules
- IS 19000:2022 effective 25 Nov 2022. Scope covers any organisation that publishes consumer reviews, including sellers collecting reviews from their own customers. [B, Business Today 13 Dec 2022: https://businesstoday.in/latest/trends/story/fake-online-reviews-bis-releases-new-standard-for-online-consumer-reviews-356195-2022-12-13 ; C, J.Sagar https://jsalaw.com/newsletters-and-updates/bis-standard-on-online-consumer-reviews]
- Voluntary status; government may make it mandatory if fake reviews persist. BIS conformity assessment scheme planned. Paid reviews may need voluntary disclosure. [B/C, conflicting on binding status]
- Not found: a mandatory BIS notification as of Oct 2026. Not found: the standard text (BIS portal not reached).

### 4. Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) for online listings
- Country-of-origin filter for e-commerce selling imported products (draft Oct/Nov 2025; final Feb 2026 per SCC Online; reported effective 1 July 2026). [C: https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2026/02/21/legal-metrology-packaged-commodities-amendment-rules-2026-explained/ ; C: https://compass.rauias.com/current-affairs/mandatory-country-origin-filter-ecommerce-platforms/ ; C: Drishti 12 Nov 2025 https://www.drishtiias.com/daily-updates/daily-news-analysis/country-of-origin-disclosure-for-e-commerce-products/print_manually ]

- Unit sale price: 2023 amendment exempts combination, group and multi-piece packs from unit sale price declaration. [C: https://www.teamleaseregtech.com/updates/article/27030/legal-metrology-packaged-commodities-amendment-rules-2023/ , 2023]
- Not found: a 2025 unit-price rule change, nor any change to MRP, net quantity or manufacturer-detail rules in the listing context. Gap: the RULES.md item on "unit price rules 2025" could not be confirmed.

## Ranking / visibility mechanics
- Not a core ranking topic, but regulatory exposure shapes listing content: country-of-origin filters (if imported) make origin a searchable attribute. [C, pattern]
- Dark-pattern rules touch listing UI: urgency badges, countdowns, "only X left" and drip-priced fees can be flagged. [PATTERN, from 2023 Guidelines list]
- Fake-review standard (IS 19000) makes review moderation and paid-review disclosure a compliance matter. [PATTERN]

## What a small new D2C seller should do / avoid (PATTERN, labelled as inference)
- Do: check MRP, net quantity, manufacturer and country-of-origin fields on every listing against the 2011 Rules and the 2026 amendment. [PATTERN]
- Do: avoid countdowns, "only N left" or other urgency claims that are not true; show all-in price at first display. [PATTERN from 2023 Guidelines]
- Do: keep TCS (0.5%) and GST registration in your planning; confirm the small-seller intra-state exemption with a CA. [PATTERN]
- Do: map DPDP obligations now, since the 18-month window ends ~May 2027. [PATTERN]
- Do: keep influencer paid-partnership labels visible, not buried in hashtags. [PATTERN, ASCI 2025]
- Avoid: buying or incentivising reviews; treat IS 19000 as the bar even while voluntary. [PATTERN]
- Avoid: relying on any single 2026 rule claim here until the gazette is checked. [PATTERN]
