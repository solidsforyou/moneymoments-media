## Key takeaways

1. **Creative is now the main targeting input on Meta (PATTERN, several sources agree).** Meta's Andromeda retrieval stage picks a shortlist of candidate ads per user, and sources say it matches on what the creative depicts. Near-identical creatives therefore give the system one option, not many. [C, multiple blogs; Meta primary post not fetched]
2. **Meta's own spokesperson says creative diversification is a long-running best practice, with no number given (FACT, secondhand).** Near-identical ads can be counted as the same ad. [B/C, Relevant Audience citing Marketing Brew; not fetched directly]
3. **Advantage+ sales campaigns (formerly "ASC") are the default for new campaigns in 2026 (FACT, single agency guide).** Meta reportedly merged manual and Advantage+ flows and made AI optimisation the default; ad sets are reportedly capped at 50 ads each. [C, 1clickreport 2026; verify in Ads Manager]
4. **Broad targeting is the common starting point, but this is vendor consensus, not a Meta document (PATTERN).** Several agency blogs recommend broad targeting plus varied creative over detailed interest stacks. Meta has not been confirmed as saying "1 to 3 ad sets" anywhere we could reach. [C]
5. **India CPM benchmarks are only available from agencies and conflict (FACT, low confidence).** upGrowth's 2026 figures: beauty Rs 180-320 CPM, fashion Rs 140-280, electronics/home Rs 120-250, Reels Rs 45-140. Other generic guides give Rs 45-350 CPM. [C, upGrowth 2026; Bosswallah 2026]
6. **India CPC and purchase-CPA ranges are also agency-only (FACT, low confidence).** upGrowth: general D2C CPC Rs 8-25, beauty Rs 15-40, purchase cost Rs 450-1,200. Regional-language creative in tier-2 cities reportedly brings CPC to Rs 4-12. [C]
7. **Mosseri's January 2025 ranking signals are watch time, likes, and sends per reach (FACT, reported by a B-rated trade site, quoting Mosseri on 22 Jan 2025 per the search summary).** Sends matter more for reaching non-followers; likes matter slightly more for connected content. The "3-5x weight for sends" multiplier is NOT verified. [B for signals, C for multiplier]
8. **Originality is a stated 2026 Instagram priority, but the enforcement details are mostly single-source (FACT for the priority; PATTERN for enforcement).** Sources say Mosseri's year-end memo (31 Dec 2025) lists "Continue to improve ranking for originality". Reposting accounts are said to lose recommendations; the "10 reposts in 30 days" threshold appears in one blog only. [C; memo not fetched]
9. **Trial Reels are reported to be available to public accounts with 1,000+ followers since July 2025 (single C-rated source).** They let you show a Reel to non-followers first and use the result to decide whether to share it with followers. [C; verify in the app]
10. **Influencer rates in India are wide and not standardised (FACT ranges, low confidence).** Nano (1K-10K) roughly Rs 2,000-8,000 per Reel; micro (10K-100K) roughly Rs 5,000-80,000; macro (100K-1M) roughly Rs 84,000-7.9 lakh. Sources disagree on tier cutoffs. [C, several agency/platform blogs]
11. **ASCI disclosure is mandatory for paid influencer content, and the Instagram "Paid partnership" tag has been accepted as an alternative since Feb 2022 (FACT).** Guidelines took effect 14 June 2021. ASCI's 2024 influencer guide reportedly recommends using the platform tag in addition to the disclosure label, not instead of it. [B for 2021 and 2022 items; A-rated 2024 guide not fetched]
12. **ASCI enforcement is active in 2025: LinkedIn advisory (Jan 2025), FY25 complaints data linking 14% of flagged ads to influencers (date not confirmed), and an Aug 2025 code update covering media companies' paid posts (FACT, B-rated press).** A 2026 law-firm post claims updated approved labels (#Ad, #Sponsored, #Paid, #Partnership) and AI-influencer rules. Not confirmed against ASCI. [B and C]
13. **Click-to-WhatsApp is growing fast globally; India-specific current data is thin (FACT for global, weak for India).** Meta's Q3 2025 results reportedly showed click-to-WhatsApp ad revenue up 60% year on year (global, B-rated). For India, the only figure found is from 2023: click-to-message ad revenue doubled year on year (Zuckerberg, Oct 2023). [B; 2023 data flagged as old]
14. **Regional-language and Hinglish creative is widely recommended, but the performance numbers come from vendors (PATTERN, not proven).** The 3-5x engagement lift and 20-36% CPA drop cited by Lapis are attributed to "Facebook India data" and "Google Think with India" without links. Treat as unverified. [C]
15. **Partnership Ads are a Meta India talking point: over 4,000 advertisers use them, and an early test reported a 14% average ROAS increase (B-rated press, date not shown).** This is not Advantage+ shopping. [B]

## Findings by sub-question

### 1. How Meta's ad system works now (Andromeda, Advantage+, creative as targeting, creative diversity, broad targeting, learning phase)

- **Andromeda is Meta's ad retrieval engine, which runs before ranking and the auction.** It narrows a very large pool of ads to a shortlist of a few thousand candidates per user. Meta's own engineering post is the primary source (Dec 2024), but it could not be fetched. Secondary reports say it uses dense embeddings to map users, contexts and creatives into one vector space. ["Explaining how Meta's Andromeda works", admetrics.io, date not shown; C] [Meta Andromeda guide, confect.io/tactics/meta-andromeda-2026, 2026; C]
- **Meta's engineering claim of a "10,000x increase in model complexity" at retrieval appears only in secondary write-ups, not in a fetched Meta document.** [C; not verified]
- **Creative as targeting (PATTERN).** Multiple analysis posts argue retrieval matches people to ads largely on what the ad depicts. Manual audience work matters less than the range and quality of creative. [Jon Loomer, "Meta Andromeda: What It Means for Your Ad Strategy", jonloomer.com/meta-andromeda/, date not shown; C, expert blog]
- **Volume alone does not help (FACT from an expert blog, C).** Uploading more creatives gives more chances to be retrieved, but Loomer warns that 50 near-duplicates are not guaranteed to work. Differentiation matters more than count. [jonloomer.com/meta-andromeda/, C]
- **Creative diversity (FACT, secondhand).** Meta's spokesperson called creative diversification a long-running best practice, gave no recommended number, and gave no date for updated guidance. Near-identical ads can be treated as the same ad. [relevantaudience.com/meta/meta-ad-creative-diversification-same-hook-same-ad/, date not shown; B/C via Marketing Brew, not fetched]
- **A Creative Diversity score (Low/Medium/High) in Ads Manager is reported as launched 26 Aug 2026 (single source, unverified).** [commonthreadco.com/blogs/coachs-corner/metas-new-creative-diversity-score-is-live-in-ads-manager-what-ecommerce-brands-must-act-on-now, C]
- **Advantage+ sales campaigns (the 2026 name for ASC).** Reported as a unified interface with AI optimisation as default, and ad sets capped at 50 ads each. [1clickreport.com/blog/meta-advantage-plus-campaign-setup-2026, 2026; C]
- **Reported performance claims (not verified).** One guide: 32% ROAS increase and 17% lower CPA vs manual. Another source cites a hedged Meta view: "improved cost per action and higher conversion rates". A Meta global study is reported to show about 12% lower cost per purchase for Advantage+ shopping vs business-as-usual ads. Meta's own caveat: results are estimates and not guaranteed. [1clickreport, C; admanage.ai / cxotoday coverage, B/C; the 12% figure is from a search summary of an unnamed study, unverified]
- **Advantage+ can underperform for niche or high-ticket products (PATTERN).** A guide notes that some advertisers with niche audiences or high-priced products still do better with tightly controlled manual campaigns. [search summary, echai.ventures / admanage, C]
- **Broad targeting (PATTERN, consensus of vendors).** Several blogs say broad targeting with Advantage+ audiences is now the default starting point. Adligator (2026) and alexneiman.com (2026) are examples. [C]
- **Learning phase: not found.** No 2026 Meta guidance on learning-phase thresholds was found. One vendor blog says consolidated structures pool results so ad sets exit learning sooner. [C; unverified]

  - CPC: general D2C Rs 8-25; beauty/skincare Rs 15-40; regional-language creative in tier-2 reportedly Rs 4-12.
  - Cost per purchase (direct-to-checkout): Rs 450-1,200; electronics/home Rs 500-1,400.
  - WhatsApp lead-form CPL: Rs 120-280.

Photo-ad CPA Rs 200-1,000.

The only specific figures (3-5x engagement, 20-36% CPA drop) are from Lapis and are not linked to a checkable report. [getkoro.app/blog/ai-ugc-video-in-hinglish-voice-lipsync-guide-for-indian-brands-2026, 2026; C] [trylapis.com/resources/indian-language-ai-ads-guide, C]

- **AI-generated UGC in Hinglish (C, vendor).** Vendors promote AI UGC and lipsync video for Indian brands. No independent D2C case study with Meta metrics was found. A Mumbai wellness brand example is cited with no figures. [getkoro.app, C]

### 4. Instagram organic algorithm, 2025-2026

- **Mosseri's January 2025 ranking signals (FACT; B-rated).** "The top three signals that matter most for ranking are watch time, likes and sends." He also said to look at average watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach in Insights. Dated 22 Jan 2025 per the search summary. [socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-2025/738034/, Jan 2025; B; page not fetched]
- **Watch time has two parts (FACT, C-rated paraphrase).** Relative watch time (share of the video watched) and absolute watch time (seconds). [creatorflow.so/blog/instagram-algorithm/, 2026; C]
- **Sends and non-followers (FACT, C-rated quote).** Likes are "slightly more important for connected content, and sends are slightly more important for unconnected content." [creatorflow.so, C]
- **Sends per reach definition (FACT, B-rated).** Out of everyone who saw the post, how many sent it to a friend by DM. [searchenginejournal.com/instagram-algorithm-shift-why-sends-matter-more-than-ever/521389, B]
- **The 3-5x weight for sends over likes (NOT VERIFIED).** Several blogs say this; no primary source found, and it conflicts with the "slightly more" wording above. Do not use this number.
- **Originality (FACT for priority; C for enforcement).** Mosseri's year-end memo (31 Dec 2025) reportedly lists "Continue to improve ranking for originality." [eclincher.com/articles/how-the-instagram-algorithm-works-in-2026, C; memo not fetched]
- **Originality enforcement details (single-source or unverified).**
  - Enforcement applies to photos and carousels from 30 Apr 2026, not just Reels. [C; one guide]
  - Accounts mostly posting others' content are removed from recommendations. [C]
  - "10 or more reposts in 30 days" excludes an account from Explore and Reels. [C; one source only, unverified]
  - AI-generated persons without the Instagram label get limited reach from 31 Aug 2026. No official Instagram announcement found for this date. [C; unverified]
- **Trial Reels (C, single source).** Public accounts with 1,000+ followers, since July 2025, can publish to non-followers first. Verify the eligibility rule in-app.
- **Reels length for non-follower recommendation (C, single source).** Reels up to 3 minutes can be recommended to non-followers. Unverified.
- **Ranking differs by surface (C).** Feed, Reels, Stories and Explore weigh signals differently; no clean Reels-specific breakdown of Mosseri's remarks was found.

## Ranking / visibility mechanics (how things show up and why)

- **Paid (PATTERN from several sources).** Meta chooses ads in stages: Andromeda retrieval shortlists candidates, then ranking and the auction pick the ad to show. Retrieval depends heavily on creative, so visually and conceptually distinct creatives get separate chances. Few near-duplicates means one candidate. [admetrics.io; Jon Loomer; C]
- **Paid (PATTERN).** Algorithmic campaigns (Advantage+) pool budget and learning across creatives and placements. Results depend on enough conversion volume; small brands can struggle to exit learning. [C; no official threshold found]
- **Organic (FACT, B).** Watch time, likes and sends per reach drive Reels distribution. Sends and watch time matter most for non-followers, which is how new viewers find you. [socialmediatoday, Jan 2025]
- **Organic (FACT, C for specifics).** Originality is now an explicit Instagram ranking goal for 2026. Reposted or AI-looking content is reported to lose reach. [eclincher, C; one-source specifics]
- **Organic (PATTERN).** Trial Reels let a new account test a Reel on non-followers before showing it to followers. [C]

## What a small new D2C seller should do / avoid (PATTERN, labelled)

**Do:**
- Build 5-8 distinct creative concepts (different hook, format and angle), not 50 variants of one concept. Keep the wording in the ad, not just the audience settings. [PATTERN from Andromeda analysis; C]
- Start with Advantage+ sales campaigns and broad targeting only if purchase volume is high enough. For a new brand with low volume, consider a tightly controlled manual campaign first. [PATTERN; C]
- Use Hinglish or regional-language versions of the best-performing creative and test them against the English version in your own account. Do not assume the vendor lift figures. [PATTERN; unverified numbers]
- Budget with your own account data. Use the India CPM/CPC ranges above only as a starting band. [PATTERN]
- Post originals: founder-shot, real product use, your own voice. Avoid reposting other creators' content as Reels. [PATTERN from originality statements; C]
- Test Trial Reels only after checking eligibility in-app (the 1,000+ follower rule is single-source). [C]
- Disclose every paid or gifted creator post with an ASCI label (for example "#Ad" or "Paid partnership" plus a clear label) in the caption or on the image. Do not rely on the platform tag alone. [B; ASCI guide not fetched]
- Pick influencers by engagement and audience fit, not follower count. Negotiate bundles of 3+ posts. [PATTERN; C]
- Use click-to-WhatsApp where a sale or support conversation needs a human. Check Meta's current India WhatsApp ad terms before you build a funnel on it. [PATTERN; B for global trend]

**Avoid:**
- Believing the 3-5x sends multiplier or the 10-repost cutoff without checking the primary source. [unverified]
- Using US or global CPM benchmarks for India: one source says clients doing so over-budget 2-3x. [C; single-source]
- Buying influencer posts from platforms with unknown pricing without a written rate card. [PATTERN]
- Posting undisclosed paid content. ASCI complaints are rising and the 2025 rules target media handles too. [B]
