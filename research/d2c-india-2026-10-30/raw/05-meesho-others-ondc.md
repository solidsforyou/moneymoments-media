## Key takeaways

1. Meesho is the largest of these by order volume. Q3 FY26 (Oct-Dec 2025, its first quarter as a listed company): operating revenue Rs 3,517.6 cr (+31.6% YoY), placed orders 690 mn (+~36%), NMV Rs 10,995 cr (+26%), net loss Rs 490 cr vs Rs 37 cr a year earlier (13x). [B]
2. Meesho listed on NSE/BSE on 10 Dec 2025 at Rs 111 issue price, opened about 46% higher (listing at Rs 162.5). IPO was about 79x subscribed. [B, Inc42/Entrackr snippets]
3. Meesho charges no commission, but sellers pay shipping/logistics (weight, zone, payment mode), and ads, value-added services and promoted listings are its monetisation. Ad spend was 2.4% of NMV in Q3 FY26 vs 1.3% a year earlier (spend, not ad revenue). [B snippet, Storyboard18; C on fee mechanics]
4. Valmo (Meesho's logistics arm) handled roughly 62% of Meesho orders in FY25 (one source) and about two-thirds of shipped orders in 2QFY26 (JM Financial, cited in a secondary piece). Meesho set up Valmo Transportation Pvt Ltd as a subsidiary on 28 Jan 2026. [B/C mixed]
5. Meesho's ranking formula is not published. Feed-driven discovery, ratings, return rate and dispatch SLA are reported as signals, but only by blogs and vendor guides (C). No source gives weights. Treat the mechanics as unverified.
6. Meesho's buyer is overwhelmingly outside metros: sources put non-metro share of orders at about 80% to over 85%, and the price band is low (AOV estimates range from Rs 274 to about Rs 610, conflicting; the company does not disclose one figure). [C, conflicting]
7. Meesho's reported loss is the main risk signal. Management says it will not add a platform fee. A 2026 seller guide (C) says high-return sellers lose visibility and pay return shipping.
8. Reliance: Reliance Retail Q1 FY27 (Apr-Jun 2026) revenue grew 7.4% to Rs 90,408 cr, profit fell 14.2%. AJIO Rush orders reportedly rose 136% QoQ. JioMart reached about 5,500 pin codes. No Ajio GMV or fee schedule was found. [B snippet]
9. Nykaa is profitable and growing. FY26 revenue crossed Rs 10,000 cr (+26%), and Q1 FY27 revenue was Rs 2,782 cr (+29.1%). Fashion is still a small part of the business (Q1 FY27 fashion revenue about Rs 252 cr, +~50%, per one aggregator). [B/C snippet, figures need checking]
10. FirstCry (Brainbees) Q1 FY27 (Apr-Jun 2026): revenue Rs 2,106 cr (+13.1%), net loss about Rs 44 cr (-34%), consolidated GMV about Rs 2,807 cr. Sources disagree on EBITDA and total revenue. [B snippet]
11. Tata Digital posted a loss of about Rs 5,000 cr in FY26, mainly from BigBasket. Tata CLiQ sits inside Tata Digital; no 2026 Tata CLiQ fashion-marketplace data was found. [B snippet, Inc42]
12. ONDC: no authoritative 2026 GMV or order total was found. Seller counts conflict across sources (3 lakh+ in a vendor blog, 7.6 lakh+ in an India Post explainer, 6.38 lakh in an older Inc42 piece). One 2026 blog says ONDC is about 3% of e-commerce GMV. Treat ONDC as small and unproven for a fashion/D2C seller. [C, conflicting]
13. Snapdeal: no 2026 news found. Search returned only 2017 coverage. Treat as not found.
14. For a small new D2C seller on Amazon, Meesho is the only one of these platforms where the fee structure is materially different (no commission). Its economics depend on logistics cost, returns and ad spend. Ajio, Nykaa, Tata CLiQ and FirstCry seller fee and onboarding terms were not found in this run.

### B. Meesho: zero-commission model and logistics

- Meesho charges no commission. Monetisation is via shipping/logistics charges to sellers, advertising and promoted listings, and value-added services. [C, several blogs agree; Inc42 feature "Decoding Meesho's Zero-Commission Model", https://inc42.com/features/decoding-meesho-business-model-seller-centric-revenue-engine/ (date not in snippet)]
- Seller shipping charges depend on product weight, delivery zone and payment mode. [C, Shiprocket guide, https://www.shiprocket.in/blog/meesho-shipping-charges/ (date not in snippet)]
- Valmo handled 62% of Meesho orders in FY25 (one source) and 61.98% of shipments in Q1 FY26 (another); JM Financial estimated about 66% of shipped orders in 2QFY26. [B/C, figures conflict slightly; cited in secondary coverage; see IPO Central https://ipocentral.in/how-meesho-makes-money-without-charging-a-commission/ (n/d)]
- Valmo Transportation Private Limited incorporated as a wholly owned subsidiary on 28 Jan 2026 to act as a logistics provider. The IPO filing mentions extending Valmo to non-Meesho merchants; one source calls that small today. [B/C, secondary]
- CEO Vidit Aatrey said there will be no platform fee going forward. [C, secondary snippet, date unknown]
- Risk flagged by one analysis: if logistics costs rise or ad monetisation plateaus, the zero-commission promise could pressure margins. [C, opinion]
- Meesho said it is not entering quick commerce (cofounder Sanjeev Barnwal; Outlook Business interview). [B/C, date not in snippet]

### C. Meesho: feed and ranking (mechanics, labelled)

- Discovery is described as feed-driven rather than search-driven. New catalogues get a probationary burst of impressions; converting that traffic earns a steady slot in the feed. [C, single vendor guide, "How to Sell on Meesho 2026", https://indiapost.org/how-to-sell-on-meesho (2026 per title; vendor)]
- Products with 4+ star ratings appear higher in search. [C, logistics blog; one source]
- Pack-and-ship SLA adherence and return rate feed into supplier ranking and catalogue visibility. A quality-check rejection or late dispatches cost more than one order. [C, vendor seller guide; "a 2026 seller guide", https://www.evanik.ai/blog/meesho-seller-guide-2026-registration-fees-tips]
- High-return sellers face reduced visibility and possible account restrictions; return shipping is charged to the seller by weight. [C, same vendor guide]
- Meesho's own job postings say its ranking systems balance price, quality and user preferences. [B-/A-ish, a job posting is company text, not a methodology doc; no weights given]
- Meesho Ads run cost-per-click inside feed and search, managed from the supplier panel. [C]
- No source gives relative weights for price, rating, returns or catalogue quality. No official Meesho ranking doc was found. Status: UNVERIFIED.

## Ranking / visibility mechanics (how things show up and why)

- Meesho (C, unverified): discovery is feed-first, not keyword-first. The early impression burst on a new listing matters. Ratings (4+ stars), return rate and dispatch SLA are said to feed supplier ranking. Ads are cost-per-click and inside the feed. Relative weights are not published.
- Meesho (B): the company says its ranking balances price, quality and user preference (job posting text). No detail.
- Ajio, Nykaa, Tata CLiQ, FirstCry, Snapdeal: no sourced ranking mechanics found in this run.
- ONDC (C): ranking is decided by each buyer app, not by one central marketplace. That is a structural point from ONDC's design; not verified with a 2026 source here.

## What a small new D2C seller should do / avoid (pattern, labelled)

PATTERN (my inference from the facts above, not a sourced rule):

- Meesho's no-commission model shifts cost into logistics, returns and ads. Model your landed cost (shipping by weight and zone, return shipping, ad spend at 2-3% of NMV as the Q3 FY26 benchmark) before comparing it with a commission-based marketplace.
- Keep returns low. Multiple C-grade sources say return rate and dispatch SLA affect visibility. Avoid thin listings that invite returns. Price low, but do not price so low that margin disappears after shipping and returns.
- Meesho's buyer is price-sensitive and non-metro. A premium D2C brand priced at Rs 1,000+ will be a poor fit unless the category is proven there. Test with a small SKU set before scaling.
- ONDC: do not build a launch plan on it. The 2026 volume data is not verified and seller counts conflict.
- Do not rely on the Snapdeal or Tata CLiQ channels for a 2026 plan; no current data was found.
- Nykaa and Ajio are better suited to brand-led, curated positioning, but no fee or buyer data was found in this run. Check their seller onboarding directly.
