## What a small new D2C seller should do / avoid (pattern, labelled)

Pattern, based on the facts above. Not a sourced recommendation.

- Do: build a repeat-purchase product (replenishable, high-frequency) before scaling paid acquisition. The ClickPost category data favours this.
- Do: track CM2 per channel and per order from the start. Investors and funders now ask for it.
- Do: treat marketplace sales as paid reach, not as the owned customer base. Capture first-party data where possible.
- Do: test Tier II and III demand. Growth is shifting there, per Unicommerce.
- Do: if you plan offline, measure store-level profitability. CBRE says brands now prioritise it.
- Avoid: relying on Meta alone. Concentration on one paid channel is exposed to CPM and CAC inflation (Meta-dependence figure still unverified).
- Avoid: assuming offline stores lower CAC without measuring it.
- Avoid: planning around a 2021-style valuation or roll-up exit. Exits are now mainly to strategic buyers.
- Avoid: scaling faster than the team and the repeat base. The Ayurveda Co closure cites exactly this. Single case, co-founder's account.
