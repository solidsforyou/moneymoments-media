## Key takeaways

1. **Fashion commission went to zero in July 2026 (FACT, single-outlet snippets).** Sellers report that from 8 Jul 2026 all fashion items carry no sales commission, after Flipkart removed an earlier Rs 1,000 price cap. Shipping and collection fees still apply. [B/C, see Findings]
2. **Shopsy is the zero-commission channel (FACT, C).** Shopsy launched a zero-commission model in Aug 2021. A 2026 seller guide lists Shopsy at 0% and Flipkart at 3-25%. The 3-25% figure is a blog claim, not a Flipkart rate card.
3. **Penalties are getting tighter (FACT, one search summary, article not identified).** Flipkart reportedly brought in order-level penalties for fulfilment lapses from 23 Aug 2026, for sellers on the platform 3+ months. Verify in Seller Hub before relying on it.
4. **Flipkart ranking is tier- and performance-led (FACT on tiers, C on mechanics).** Gold, Silver and Bronze tiers are set on a rolling 90-day window (sales, fulfilment, cancellations, returns). Higher tiers get better placement, lower fees, and priority deal slots. No official Flipkart ranking document was found.
5. **Late dispatch, defects and incomplete listings hurt visibility (C, several vendor blogs agree).** Late-dispatch and order-defect rates feed into search placement; incomplete listings are described as effectively invisible.
6. **Flipkart Ads are the paid lever (FACT, B for July 2025 launch).** Formats are Product Listing Ads (PLA, search) and Product Contextual Ads (PCA). In July 2025 Flipkart added a credit option where ad spend is deducted from next month's earnings. CPC ranges quoted by blogs conflict (Rs 4-15 vs Rs 2-15).
7. **Big Billion Days 2025 had no official GMV in any source I found (gap).** The sale ran from 23 Sep 2025. Post-event coverage (Coresight, 10 Oct 2025) exists, but the snippet has no GMV figure.
8. **BBD 2026 is underway, with no final numbers yet (FACT, B/C).** Early access began 8 Oct 2026, and the sale opened for shoppers on 9 Oct (YourStory headline). Flipkart reported 6.3 million concurrent users in the first hour and 287 million+ sessions in early access. Flipkart said 51% of transacting sellers did twice their normal business on early-access day (YourStory snippet).
9. **Flipkart IPO is on hold, with no DRHP filed (FACT, C/B, mixed dates).** Bloomberg-sourced reports in March 2026 said bank pitches would start in April. A Zerodha post dated 15 May 2026 says Flipkart deferred the IPO to a profitability-first strategy. A July 2026 summary says no DRHP as of July 2026. Reports say the domicile has moved back to India, a precondition for a domestic listing.
10. **Myntra M-Now is still small and Bengaluru-first at launch (FACT, B for launch, C for expansion).** M-Now launched Dec 2024 as a 30-minute fashion delivery service in Bengaluru. A 2026 guide says delivery now runs 30 min to 2 hr across Bengaluru, Delhi-NCR, Mumbai and Hyderabad.
11. **Myntra onboarding is a gated, paperwork-heavy process (FACT on documents, C on timelines).** Required documents: GST, PAN, bank details, business registration, and brand authorisation where applicable. Guides disagree on timing (4-8 weeks vs 6-12 weeks). A 2025 guide says there is no registration fee but a one-time Growth Enablement Fee is deducted from first settlements. No verified current M-Now commission schedule was found.
12. **Market share data is old (FACT, old A/B data).** The only Redseer split found is from the 2022 festive season: Flipkart Group about 62% of first-week GMV, Amazon about 26%. Meesho took 25% of orders in the 2023 festive week, up from 21%. No 2025 Redseer share split was found. Amazon disputed the 2022 report.
13. **Flipkart drew the most consumer complaints in 2025 (FACT, A for the govt dashboard).** The National Consumer Helpline logged about 1.33 lakh complaints against Flipkart, vs about 91,248 for Amazon and 29,284 for Meesho. Most are delivery and refund issues, not seller-side issues.
14. **Return fraud is pushing Flipkart to tighten buyer rules (FACT, date unknown).** Flipkart cut its fashion return window from 30 to 10 days after a spike in suspected-fraud returns, mainly winter wear. Quality audits with delisting threats are reported, with a goal of cutting returns 10-15% in a year (undated).
15. **Flipkart Minutes has no verified 2026 numbers (gap).** The only figures are from BBD 2026 coverage: order volume about 3.5x normal in the first hour, and new customer adds at 11x (one outlet says 34% for new users). No store count or GMV was verified.

## Findings by sub-question

### 1. How Flipkart search ranks listings (tiers, F-Assured, ratings, price, ads, PLA/PCA)

- Flipkart sorts sellers into Gold, Silver and Bronze tiers using a rolling 90-day performance window. Grading covers sales volume, fulfilment reliability, cancellations and returns. Some sources also mention a Platinum tier; not confirmed. [C] https://www.gonukkad.com/blog/flipkart-account-management-checklist (date not shown); see also https://wareiq.com/resources/blogs/flipkart-gold-seller-complete-guide/ (undated). Several vendor guides agree on the tier structure; no Flipkart official page was found.
- Higher tiers get better search placement, lower closing fees, and priority deal slots. Gold sellers also get invitations to events and a dedicated account manager. [C] https://www.eshopbox.com/blog/guide-to-becoming-flipkart-gold-seller (undated). Single-source per benefit; consultancies sell tier-upgrade help, so treat as commercially biased.
- Payout timelines by tier are inconsistent across sources. A 2017 MediaNama report gives 7/12/15 business days (Gold/Silver/Bronze); a newer blog gives Gold 7, Silver 10, Bronze 15. The 2017 figure is outdated. [C/old] https://www.medianama.com/2017/11/223-flipkart-sellers-policy-changes/ ; blog https://www.gonukkad.com/blog/flipkart-account-management-checklist
- Ranking inputs named by vendor guides: late-dispatch rate, order-defect rate, cancellation rate, listing completeness, conversion rate, customer rating, fulfilment speed, sales velocity. One source claims inconsistent sales are penalised. [C] https://wareiq.com/?p=7755 (F-Assured guide, 2025 per title); https://emerge.fibre2fashion.com/blogs/11209/how-to-increase-flipkart-sales-in-2026-10-proven-strategies (2026 per URL). Several sources agree on the first four; the rest are one-off claims.
- Flipkart Assured badge: vendor guides say it needs a quality audit pass and good returns and rating data. Reported (undated) that Flipkart threatens delisting or badge removal on failed audits. [C] https://www.thenewsminute.com/atom/amid-suspicions-fraud-flipkart-reduces-return-period-fashion-10-days-116098 (date not shown); badge rules not verified on an official page.
- Flipkart Ads (FACT, B): July 2025 launch of a credit-led ad option, spend deducted from the next month's earnings. Formats: PLA, PCA and other recommended campaigns. [B, Jul 2025] https://www.storyboard18.com/advertising/flipkart-rolls-out-credit-led-advertisement-initiative-to-boost-seller-visibility-75147.htm
- Flipkart's retail media deck (Jan 2025) describes CPC bidding for search placements. [C/B, Jan-Feb 2025, hosted by a third party] https://persistent.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/fcc-retail-media-solution.pdf ; a blog gives CPC Rs 4-15 by category [C] https://www.gonukkad.com/blog/flipkart-ads-worth-it, another gives Rs 2-15 [C] https://emerge.fibre2fashion.com/blogs/11136/amazon-vs-flipkart-2026-where-should-you-focus-your-product-listings (2026 per URL). Conflicting; use as a rough range only.
- Common advice: advertise about 20-30% of the catalogue and optimise organic ranking on the rest. [C, one or two blogs] https://thegstco.com/kn/blogs/ecommerce/10-proven-strategies-to-boost-your-sales-performance-on-flipkart (undated). PATTERN-level advice; not measured.
- Price as a ranking input: not found in any sourced document. "not found".

## Ranking / visibility mechanics

- **Organic search (PATTERN, C-sourced, consistent across several vendor guides):** Listing completeness and quality, sales velocity, conversion rate, customer rating, late-dispatch rate, defect rate and cancellations. Tier (Gold/Silver/Bronze) changes placement and fees. Flipkart Assured badge adds trust in some surfaces.
- **Paid (FACT, B):** PLA in search results and category pages; PCA in contextual slots; pricing per click, credit-based option since July 2025.
- **Price:** not found as a ranking factor in any sourced document.
- **Myntra:** ranking not verified; treat as unknown.
- **Shopsy:** targets social and value shoppers, 0% commission positioning; visibility mechanics not sourced.

## What a small new D2C seller should do / avoid (PATTERN, labelled)

Do:
- Check Seller Hub's current rate card before pricing. Fashion commission and Shopsy 0% are the two numbers most likely to change your margin. (FACT that they moved; PATTERN in the recommendation.)
- Keep dispatch and cancellation rates tight from day one. Vendor guides agree these feed visibility and penalties. (C, several agree.)
- Use PLA on a small subset (about 20-30% of SKUs) and measure ROAS before scaling. (C, one or two blogs; PATTERN.)
- For Myntra, get GST, PAN, bank, business registration and brand authorisation ready before applying. Expect a multi-week wait. (C, several agree on the documents.)
- Log every penalty and settlement deduction; dispute logistics-caused delays. (C.)

Avoid:
- Do not price for a 0% commission on non-fashion items, or on Flipkart at all, without checking the rate card. (PATTERN.)
- Do not rely on tier-upgrade consultancies' claims about payout days or fee discounts; sources conflict. (C.)
- Do not use the 2022 Redseer share split as current. (Old data.)
- Do not expect Flipkart IPO news to change seller economics in the short term. (PATTERN.)
