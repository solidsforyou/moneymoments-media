## 1. How things rank, everywhere (PATTERN)

No Indian platform publishes its ranking formula. Across Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho, quick
commerce, Meta, Instagram and Google, the same five layers come up again and again:

1. **Relevance gate.** You only enter the auction/feed if the system understands what you
   are: title, attributes, category, backend keywords, catalogue completeness, images. On
   Meta the creative itself is the "keywords" (Andromeda shortlists ads by what they show).
2. **Predicted conversion.** CTR, conversion rate, sales velocity, watch time / sends on
   Instagram. Every platform ranks what it expects to sell or hold attention, because that
   is what it earns on.
3. **Reliability filter.** Stock-outs, late dispatch, cancellations, high returns, low
   ratings push you down or out: Amazon Buy Box, Flipkart Gold/Silver/Bronze tiers on a
   90-day window, Meesho return rate and SLA, quick-commerce dark-store stock.
4. **Paid boost.** Ads buy the first sales, and those sales then lift organic rank.
   Amazon says ad rank weighs relevance over bid (about 92% of chosen Sponsored Products
   ads in 2024 were not the highest bid, US auction data) [B].
5. **Machine-readable answers.** AI assistants (Amazon's Rufus → Alexa for Shopping,
   Google AI Overviews/AI Mode in Hindi, ChatGPT checkout for Indian brands from Jul 2026)
   pick products whose listings answer buyers' questions: specs, size, fabric, care,
   comparisons, reviews, schema.

**Why it's shifting toward paid:** marketplaces are dropping commissions and making money on
ads and logistics instead. Amazon <₹1,000 zero referral fee, Flipkart fashion zero
commission, Meesho and Shopsy zero commission, quick-commerce ad revenue (Zepto ₹1,636 cr,
+151%). Cheaper to list, more expensive to be seen.

## 2. Amazon.in

- **Zero referral fee on items under ₹1,000** across 1,800+ categories (12.5 crore products),
  from 16 Mar 2026. Includes apparel. Was under ₹300 in 2025. Easy Ship fees cut 20%+ for
  items under ₹300. [A, verified by us: aboutamazon.in announcement, Reuters, YourStory]
- Above ₹1,000: referral = (price + shipping) × category %; official table not reached. Closing
  fee reportedly +₹1/₹3 from 7 Sep 2026; Easy Ship national rate reportedly ₹77→₹65 (Mar 2026).
  [C, check]
- 20 lakh sellers (Amazon, 8 Sep 2026) [A, company-reported].
- **GST:** online sellers must register whatever their turnover and can't use the
  Composition Scheme. TCS is 0.5% (since Jul 2024). [C, check on CBIC]
- **Brand Registry:** a pending trademark application can be enough to apply; acceptance at
  Amazon's discretion. [C]
- **Payments:** DD+7 reserve reported from 12 Mar 2026; cycle length conflicts. [C, check]
- **Rufus:** Amazon retired Rufus on 13 May 2026 in the US and replaced it with Alexa for
  Shopping. No confirmed India date; Rufus has been live in India since 2024. [B, verified by us]
- **Ranking signals most sources repeat:** sales velocity, conversion, CTR, reviews, keyword
  relevance, price, Buy Box (price + availability + on-time shipping + low negative
  feedback). "A9/A10" and the "honeymoon" for new listings are seller folklore. [C]
- **Suppression:** missing main image or product codes, thin details, banned title words.
  Check Inventory > Suppressed. [C, consensus]
- **Ads:** Amazon India ad income about ₹8,350 cr in FY25 (+24%). Amazon Marketing Cloud is
  open to all Indian sponsored-ads advertisers (Sep 2025) [A]. A free AI video generator
  (Nov 2025) and Ads Agent / Creative Agent (Mar 2026) are available [A/B]. No trustworthy India
  CPC or ACoS benchmark exists; agency numbers differ by 2-3x.
- **Events:** Great Indian Festival 2026 from 8 Oct (Prime 7 Oct); 2025 had 276 crore visits,
  over two-thirds of SMB sellers' first-48-hour orders from tier 2/3 [A/B]. Prime Day 2026
  reported 4-6 Jul. Deal fees for India not found.
- Amazon Now (quick commerce) passed $1 bn annualised (Sep 2026); Amazon Bazaar (low price) [B/C].
- Influencer programme: about 2 lakh creators, about 2,000-follower minimum [B]. Brand
  Referral Bonus: no sign it exists in India.

## 3. Flipkart group

- **Fashion is zero commission at every price from 8 Jul 2026** (cap of ₹1,000 removed), about
  90,000 sellers. Fixed fee, shipping, collection fee and 18% GST on fees still apply. [A,
  verified by us: Flipkart Stories, Inc42, Outlook Business]
- Ranking: seller tier (Gold/Silver/Bronze, rolling 90 days), dispatch, cancellations,
  defects, listing completeness, ads (PLA/PCA) [C consensus]. Order-level penalties reported
  from 23 Aug 2026 [C].
- Fashion return window cut from 30 to 10 days after return fraud [B, date unknown].
- BBD 2026: early access 8 Oct, sale 9 Oct; 51% of sellers doubled normal business on day one
  [B]. IPO deferred (May 2026).
- Most consumer complaints of any platform in 2025 (about 1.33 lakh vs Amazon 91k) [A, NCH].
- Myntra M-Now 30 min to 2 hr delivery in four cities; brand onboarding 4-12 weeks [C].

## 4. Meesho, others, ONDC

- Meesho: no commission, sellers pay shipping; money from ads (2.4% of NMV, up from 1.3%).
  Listed 10 Dec 2025; Q3 FY26 revenue ₹3,518 cr, 690 mn orders, loss ₹490 cr [B].
  About 80%+ non-metro buyers. Feed favours rating 4+, low returns, fast dispatch [C].
- Nykaa FY26 revenue above ₹10,000 cr; Ajio Rush orders reportedly +136% QoQ; FirstCry Q1 FY27
  revenue +13% [B].
- ONDC: no reliable 2026 volumes; about 3% of GMV by one blog. Not a planning channel yet [C].

## 5. Quick commerce

- About $10-13 bn GMV (FY26), forecast $90 bn by FY31 (Google-Redseer) [B]. Blinkit about
  46-49% share, profitable at adjusted EBITDA; Zepto FY26 loss ₹5,905 cr; Instamart loss.
- Commission has roughly doubled (about 12% to about 24%, 2023-26, vendor sample) [C].
  Visibility = dark-store stock + paid placement [C].
- Beauty is the clearest non-grocery winner. Fashion on quick commerce not quantified.
- Regulators are pushing back: Maharashtra suspended 14 dark-store licences; FDI
  inventory-model complaints [B].

## 6. Own website

- **COD RTO (returned undelivered) is the main cost**: COD RTO about 25-40%, prepaid about 2-3% [C].
  Partial prepaid, OTP and WhatsApp confirmation are the usual fixes; test on our own orders.
- Shopify Basic about ₹1,499/month annual + GST [C, check]. Checkout tools (GoKwik, Razorpay
  Magic) make big claims without controlled data.
- Shipping about ₹17-26 per 500 g via aggregators [C]. WhatsApp marketing message about
  ₹0.86, utility about ₹0.12 (Jan 2026); abandoned-cart often billed as marketing [C].
- UPI: 24.5 bn transactions in Aug 2026 [B].

## 7. Meta and Instagram

- Creative is targeting; near-duplicate ads count as one; go broad and make many distinct
  creatives [C consensus, Meta calls diversification a best practice].
- India CPM about ₹140-280 for fashion, Reels cheaper; purchase CPA about ₹450-1,200 [C, ranges
  differ by 2-3x]. CAC reportedly up about 32% in a year [C].
- Instagram organic: watch time, likes and **sends** are the top signals; sends matter most
  for reaching non-followers (Mosseri, Jan 2025) [B]. Originality is a 2026 priority.
- Influencers: nano about ₹2-8k per reel; ASCI label or "Paid partnership" tag mandatory [B].
- Click-to-WhatsApp ads growing fast globally; no fresh India cost data.

## 8. Google, YouTube, AI search

- AI Overviews (Aug 2024) and Hindi AI Mode (Sep 2025) in India; US studies show organic CTR
  falling 35-61% when an AI summary appears. No India study [B/C].
- Free Merchant Center listings work in India; sponsored tiles now mixed in [B/C].
- YouTube Shopping affiliate (Flipkart, Myntra, then others) since Oct 2024 [B].
- ChatGPT checkout for Indian D2C brands via GoKwik/PayU (Jul 2026) [B, check].

## 9. The shopper and the economy

- E-retail about $65 bn in 2025 (+~20%), about 290-300 mn shoppers (Bain 2026, via press) [B].
- Festive 2026: about ₹24,000 cr in the first two days; about two-thirds of shoppers from
  tier-2 and rural areas. Diwali 8 Nov 2026 [B].
- **GST 2.0 (22 Sep 2025): apparel up to ₹2,500 at 5%, above at 18%** [A via snippet].
- Gen Z about 40-45% of e-retail shoppers and about half of new orders [B]. Repo rate 5.25% since Dec 2025.

## 10. Rules to follow

- E-commerce Rules amended 2026 (reported in force 1 Jan 2027, contents unverified) [C].
- CCPA dark patterns: 13 banned practices (false urgency, drip pricing, basket sneaking...);
  platforms asked to self-audit (Jun 2025) [B]. Don't use fake timers or fake "only 2 left".
- Legal Metrology: MRP, net quantity, country of origin, manufacturer details on listings;
  country-of-origin filter for imports (2026) [C].
- No QCO on finished apparel found; several fibre QCOs withdrawn in Nov 2025 [B/C].
- DPDP Rules notified Nov 2025; main duties from about May 2027 [B].
- ASCI influencer rules updated Apr 2025 [A].

## 11. What this means for our trade (PATTERN, for the founder to decide)

Our stock is tees and joggers (memory #32); selling prices aren't in memory yet. If an item
sells under ₹1,000:
- Amazon.in: no referral fee; we'd pay closing, shipping and weight fees.
- Flipkart fashion: zero commission at any price.
- GST 5% up to ₹2,500.
Being listed is now cheap; being seen is what costs. The things that decide rank and that we
control: complete listings that answer questions (size chart, fabric, fit, care), reviews,
staying in stock, fast dispatch and low returns. Fashion returns (reportedly 25-40%) are the
biggest risk to both margin and rank, so the size chart and accurate photos come first.
