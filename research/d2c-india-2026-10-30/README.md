## 1. How things rank, everywhere (PATTERN)

No Indian platform publishes a ranking formula (Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho and quick commerce
all checked). Across all of them the same five layers keep coming back:

1. **Relevance gate.** The system has to understand what you sell: title, attributes,
   category, keywords, images. On Meta, ad volume and variety feed the candidate pool
   (Andromeda narrows tens of millions of ads to a few thousand per person) [official].
2. **Predicted conversion or attention.** Clicks, conversion, sales speed; on Instagram
   Reels the predictions are reshares, full watches, likes and audio-page visits [official,
   Mosseri 2023]. Platforms show what they expect to earn from.
3. **Reliability filter.** Stock-outs, late dispatch, cancellations, returns, ratings.
   Amazon's Buy Box needs "performance-based requirements" and a competitive price helps
   [official, metrics not listed]. Flipkart now fines late dispatch and cancellations per
   order [press]. Meesho ranks with AI models on quality and returns [press, weights unpublished].
4. **Paid boost.** Ads buy the first sales, which then feed organic rank. Amazon says ad rank
   weighs relevance over bid: about 92% of chosen Sponsored Products ads in 2024 were not
   the highest bid [official, US context, Amazon's FTC response]. Meesho's ads auto-optimise
   for return on spend; sellers don't pick keywords [press].
5. **Content that machines can answer from.** Rufus is live for all Amazon.in customers,
   1 crore+ users [official]; Alexa+ is in early access in India in English, Hindi and Hinglish
   [official]; Google AI Overviews (Aug 2024) and AI Mode in Hindi (Sep 2025) [official]; Indian
   brands can sell inside ChatGPT via GoKwik/PayU (Jul 2026) [press]. They all pull from listing
   detail: size, fabric, fit, care, comparisons, reviews.

**The shift:** marketplaces are dropping commission and earning from ads and logistics:
Amazon zero referral under ₹1,000, Flipkart zero commission on fashion, Shopsy and Meesho zero
commission, Blinkit buying stock wholesale, Zepto ad revenue ₹1,636 cr (+151%). Listing is
cheap; being seen is what costs.

## 2. Amazon.in

**Fees** [official, sell.amazon.in fee schedule, read 31 Oct 2026]
- **Zero referral fee on items up to ₹1,000** in 1,800+ categories (12.5 crore products), from
  16 Mar 2026; t-shirts named in Amazon's release. (2025: under ₹300 only.)
- **Above ₹1,000 the whole price is charged the category rate**, not just the part above.
  Apparel rates: Men's T-shirts 23%, Shorts 24%, Shirts 21%, Other Products 19%, Dress 19%,
  Sleepwear 19%, Women's innerwear 18%, Ethnic 16.5%, Sarees 15%, Baby 7%. Which category
  joggers and women's tees fall under is not confirmed (probably "Other Products", 19%).

  | Men's tee price | Referral at 23% | Left before other fees |
  |---|---|---|
  | ₹999 | ₹0 | ₹999 |
  | ₹1,001 | ₹230.23 | ₹770.77 |
  | ₹1,299 | ₹298.77 | ₹1,000.23 |
  | ₹1,499 | ₹344.77 | ₹1,154.23 |

  A tee has to sell at about ₹1,300 to keep what it keeps at ₹999.
- **Closing fees:** official examples include Self Ship ₹499 shirt → ₹26, Self Ship ₹299 → ₹20,
  FC ₹249 → ₹27; +₹1 (up to ₹500) / +₹3 (above) from 7 Sep 2026 for FBA, Easy Ship and Seller
  Flex. Amazon's own anchor: a ₹299 t-shirt on Easy Ship pays ₹56 in all fees.
- **FBA:** pick and pack ₹17 up to 1 kg; storage ₹50 per cubic foot per month. FBA gives the
  Prime badge and improves Buy Box chances [official].
- **Weight-handling (shipping) tables are images on a blocked host**: still unconfirmed.
- **Payments:** paid 7 days after delivery, deposited every 7 days [official].
- **Brand Registry:** a pending trademark application is enough; free; the trademark owner
  enrols; you don't have to sell on Amazon first [official].
- **GST:** online sellers can't use the Composition Scheme [official, CGST text]; must register
  to sell inter-state whatever the turnover; TCS 0.5% [press].
- Programmes: Karigar, Saheli, Saheli Accelerate, Launchpad exist [official]; criteria not read.

**Visibility**
- No official ranking formula; "A9/A10" and the new-listing "honeymoon" are seller folklore.
- A+ Content and Brand Registry are sold as conversion tools ("up to 8%" basic, "up to 20%"
  premium A+), not ranking boosts [official, promotional].
- Amazon.in suggests 10+ listings and ₹5,000+ ad spend to get going [official, promotional].
- Not found anywhere official: how Amazon's Choice and Best Seller badges are awarded, COSMO
  details, India-specific Buy Box metrics.

**Ads and events**
- Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands, Stores, Sponsored Display on Amazon.in, pay per click
  [official]. Amazon Marketing Cloud open to all Indian sponsored-ads advertisers (Sep 2025);
  AI image generation in India from 6 Jul 2026 [official].
- **No official India CPC or ACoS benchmark exists.** Use our own first 30-60 days.
- Brand Referral Bonus is US-only [official]. No deal or coupon fee published for India.
- Great Indian Festival 2025 (22-23 Sep): 276 crore visits, 70% of traffic and two-thirds of
  SMB sellers from tier 2/3 [official]. Prime Day 2025 and 2026 were Prime-only [official].
- Amazon Now (quick commerce) in 120+ cities (Oct 2026) [press].

## 3. Flipkart group

- **Fashion: zero commission at every price from 8 Jul 2026**, about 90,000 sellers [official,
  Flipkart release]. Fixed, collection and shipping fees still apply; amounts not confirmed
  (seller.flipkart.com kept returning "site overloaded").
- **Shopsy: zero commission on every product** [press].
- **Penalties from 23 Aug 2026, per shipment:** ₹30 missed dispatch date, ₹60 seller
  cancellation, ₹90 late then cancelled; 3 missed dates = 1 cancellation; new sellers exempt
  for 3 months [press, PTI and Inc42].
- Flipkart Ads: "Advertise Now, Pay Later" (no interest, deducted next month) and Saarthi, a
  free AI campaign tool (Aug 2026) [official]. CPCs unconfirmed.
- Flipkart Minutes: about 1,200 micro-fulfilment centres, 150+ cities, about 500 D2C brands,
  60% repeat customers [official, company-reported].
- Big Billion Days 2026: orders, units, buyers each up about 20-22% over 11 days; no GMV [official].
- **Myntra Rising Stars:** zero commission for new D2C brands that sell mainly through their
  own site or social [press]. Myntra's normal commission unconfirmed (blogs: 4-30%).

## 4. Meesho and others

## 7. Meta and Instagram

- Andromeda: tens of millions of ads → a few thousand candidates per person → ranking [official].
  More distinct ads = bigger pool. "Creative is the targeting" is agency opinion.
- Advantage+ sales campaigns replace the old shopping campaigns; moving a running campaign
  into Advantage+ restarts learning, no workaround [official]. Meta's "22% higher ROAS" is
  Meta's own marketing.
- **Originality rule (30 Apr 2026):** accounts that mostly repost other people's photos,
  carousels or Reels can't be recommended until most of their last 30 days is original [official].
- **AI profiles (31 Aug 2026):** showing an AI person without the "AI-generated profile" label
  cuts reach [official].
- Shoppable Reels: tag up to 30 products per Reel, catalogue or link (Mar 2026) [official];
  India coverage wording unclear.
- **ASCI (2024 guide):** visible "Ad" label up front, not buried in hashtags, not bio-only;
  Instagram's Paid partnership tag goes alongside it [official].
- India CPM/CPA, the learning-phase threshold and Hinglish lift: all unconfirmed.

## 9. Shoppers and the economy

- E-retail 2025: $65-66 bn (+19-21%), 290-300 mn shoppers; Gen Z 40-45% of shoppers and about
  half of new orders; $170-180 bn by 2030 [official, Bain 2026].
- Redseer: H1 2026 about $38 bn (+25%); full 2026 $90 bn+; festive 2026 forecast $15-16 bn
  (+~25%), 180-185 mn shoppers [official].
- Festive 2026 opening: about ₹24,000 cr in two days, two-thirds of shoppers from tier 2 and
  rural; fashion 15-18% of sales, growing 22-25% [press].
- **GST: apparel and footwear 5% up to ₹2,500, 18% above** (since 22 Sep 2025) [press].
- **RBI raised the repo rate to 5.50% on 7 Oct 2026** (first rise since Feb 2023); CPI 5.54% in
  Sep 2026 (new 2024 base) [press].
- Courier export cap (₹10 lakh per shipment) removed from 1 Apr 2026 [press].

## 10. Indian D2C now

- Funding 2025 about $898 mn (+9%); 2022 peak about $1.6 bn; 15 D2C IPOs and 105 acquisitions
  since 2021 [press, Tracxn].
- Q1 FY27: Lenskart revenue ₹2,714 cr (+43%), Honasa ₹756 cr (+27%, profit +119%) [press].
- Apparel: Snitch FY26 about ₹900 cr (+80%), 60% online / 40% stores, thin profit; Bewakoof
  FY26 ₹243 cr, loss ₹87 cr; The Souled Store FY25 ₹492 cr [press].
- D2C brands took about 28% of retail leasing in H1 2026, fashion about 69% of that [official, CBRE].

## 11. Rules

- E-commerce Rules amendment (notified 9 Sep 2026), in force 1 Jan 2027: grievance officer,
  48-hour acknowledgement, one-month resolution, legal identity on the store [press].
- Dark patterns are being fined in 2026 (₹1-7 lakh, e.g. PhysicsWallah, Zepto) [press]: no fake
  timers, no fake "only 2 left".
- Country-of-origin filter for imported goods from 1 Jul 2026 [press].
- No quality-control order on finished apparel; a textile labelling draft (Jul 2026) is pending
  [press]. Footwear QCO legacy stock allowed to 31 Jul 2027 [official, gazette].
- DPDP data rules: main duties from about May 2027 [press]. ASCI rules above [official].
- FDI: inventory model allowed for exports only (Jul 2026) [press]. CCI case against Amazon and
  Flipkart now in Karnataka High Court, no ruling [press].

## 12. What this means for our trade (PATTERN, for the founder to decide)

Our stock is tees and joggers (memory #32). Selling prices aren't in memory yet.
- **Price at ₹999 or below on Amazon.in** and we pay no referral fee; one rupee over ₹1,000 and
  a men's tee pays 23% of the whole price. The same goes for any marketplace with a ₹1,000 line.
- On Flipkart, fashion pays no commission at any price, so ₹1,000+ items are cheaper to sell
  there than on Amazon. Myntra Rising Stars may be free for us as a new D2C brand.
- GST stays 5% up to ₹2,500.
- What decides rank that we control: complete listings that answer questions (size chart,
  fabric, fit, care, photos), reviews, staying in stock, dispatching on time (Flipkart now fines
  per order), and low returns. Fashion returns are the biggest risk to margin and rank.
