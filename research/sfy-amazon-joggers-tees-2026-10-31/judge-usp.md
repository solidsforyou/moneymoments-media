## 2. USP pairs for Approach A

| Pair | Clarity | Difference | Complaints | Provability | Risk | Total /50 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. 100% Cotton + Heavyweight | 7 | 6 | 6 | 3 | 3 | 25 |
| 2. 100% Cotton + Pre-shrunk | 7 | 7 | 8 | 5 | 4 | 31 |
| 3. Heavyweight + Won't shrink | 7 | 8 | 9 | 4 | 5 | **33** |
| 4. 100% Cotton + Oversized/relaxed | 8 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 5 | 25 |

Evidence per score:
- 1. Clarity: two short words, but H: GSM "can't feel through a phone". Difference: R s8 Veirdo and
  Souled both say pure cotton; DaMENSCH sells "Heavyweight" (R update) but no one states GSM.
  Complaints: answers thin fabric (R s6 lines 60, 145), not shrinking/fading. Provability: burn test
  only, GSM not measured (H founder answers). Risk: two unproven claims (R: Veirdo is pure cotton and
  still gets fading complaints, so the cotton claim does not protect it).
- 2. Clarity: familiar words. Difference: no competitor in the files claims pre-shrunk. Complaints:
  shrinking is the Van Heusen complaint ("significant shrinkage after one wash", R s3 jogger notes);
  fading not answered. Provability: pre-shrunk is a process fact (founder: yes) but cotton has no lab
  proof. Risk: one weak claim.
- 3. Clarity: "won't shrink" is the plainest buyer language; "heavyweight" is a feel word. Difference:
  neither is stated by Souled, Veirdo, Jockey, Van Heusen. Complaints: thin plus shrinking, the two
  most fixable. Provability: pre-shrunk yes; GSM cheap to measure but not yet done. Risk: avoids the
  unproven "100% cotton" claim; absolute "won't shrink" is overreach, "pre-shrunk" is safe (cotton still
  shrinks a little).
- 4. Clarity: fit is visible in the photo. Difference: Souled, Veirdo, Snitch, DaMENSCH all oversized;
  baggy is common (R s2 v5). Complaints: fit 11 but S-L only and no shrink answer. Provability: fit
  visible, cotton unproven. Risk: "100% cotton" unproven.

**Winner: Pair 3 (Heavyweight + Pre-shrunk), 33.** Deciding reason: it is the only pair that avoids
the unproven "100% cotton" claim while answering the two complaints competitors leave open (thin
fabric, shrinking). Condition: measure GSM first, and word it as "pre-shrunk", not "won't shrink".

**Where each pair is weaker.** 1: two unprovable claims, nothing on shrinking. 2: cotton claim is
common and unproven. 3: GSM is unmeasured today and heavyweight is hard to see on a phone. 4: no
differentiation, no complaint answered.

No better pair added: the evidence does not show a provable third angle (fading, price, fit
photo-proof all lack backing in the files).

## 3. Hybrid

Applies A's rule to each image rather than to the whole set: every image carries at most 2 USPs, but
the 7 slots are used so that Heavyweight and Pre-shrunk lead, then one slot each for fabric next to a
regular tee or scale (GSM proof), care/wash (one image), and size/model height with "S-L only"
(H fixes 2, 5, 8, 9). Education stays in the description. Scored: Clarity 8, Difference 8, Complaints
9, Provability 4 now (7 after a GSM measurement and a lab fibre test), Risk 6 = 35 (38 after proof).
It beats both A (33) and B (31) on the evidence: H's "what would make me buy" list needs proof images
but not on one crowded panel. Ship it only once GSM is measured; before that, use Pair 3 images
without the GSM proof slot.
