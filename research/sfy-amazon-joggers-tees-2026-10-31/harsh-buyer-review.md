## 4. Harsh feedback

**A. First 3 seconds**
- Main images (product on white) are sharp and correct for Amazon. Best part of the kit.
- Then the model shots: same smooth face, same hair, same pose in every colour and every
  product. It reads as AI. The women's set is the same three faces too.
- Joggers main image: I see **wide-leg pants**, not joggers. No cuff. If I searched "joggers"
  I'd think I clicked the wrong thing.
- Would I scroll past? No, it stops me. Whether it converts is the problem.

**B. Headline and promise**
- "FIT & FABRIC." and "EVERY STYLE STARTS WITH THE ESSENTIALS." say nothing I care about.
- "TRUE 100% COTTON": "TRUE" makes me wonder what fake 100% looks like. Prove it, don't shout it.

**C. Claims, word for word**
- "100% COTTON", "320 GSM", "230 GSM": believable only with a spec sheet or tag photo. I'd check.
- "BRUSHED FLEECE INTERIOR" (joggers): fleece makes me think polyester. Say cotton fleece or
  I'll assume it's a blend, which kills the "100% cotton" claim.
- "BREATHABLE", "DURABLE", "ALL-DAY COMFORT", "SOFT": every listing says these. Noise.
- "PREMIUM COTTON" (oversized size-chart banner): vague.
- "RELAXED WIDE LEG" + "STRAIGHT OPEN HEM" vs folder name and banner "JOGGERS": conflict.
- "SIZE GUIDE (UNISEX)" on the fitted women's and men's tees: a fitted tee is not unisex.
  Conflicts with the separate men's/women's charts.
- No claim about shrinkage, colour fastness or wash care, the two things I've been burned by.

**D. Visuals and layout**
- Infographic icon row at the bottom ("100% COTTON, 320 GSM, BREATHABLE, DURABLE, ALL-DAY
  COMFORT") is unreadable at phone size. Smallest readable text on page 5 is the panel labels.
- Size codes "FS FM FL MS MM ML" mean nothing to me. Amazon's size picker will say S/M/L; the
  chart must match.
- Colour palette is consistent (black, white, royal blue, brand blue). Good.
- The blue is very saturated. I'd expect it to look different in real life.
- Page 6 lifestyle is the same man against the same wall in every colour: looks templated.
- Count of separate things to read on page 5: 15+. Too many.

**E. Product proof**
- No real photo of the actual garment, tag or label. The neck print "SOLIDS FOR YOU" looks
  rendered.
- No fabric next to a coin, ruler or regular tee to show "heavyweight".
- Jogger size chart has waist and length, but no inseam, thigh or rise for a wide-leg pant.
- Missing: XL and above, wash care, shrinkage, model's height and size worn.

**F. Experience**
- I expect a heavy, warm, roomy fit. Appealing for winter. 320 GSM wide-leg cotton in a Delhi
  May? Too hot. Nothing says what season it's for.
- "Brushed fleece" + 320 GSM sounds like it'll pill. Tell me it won't.

**G. Price and value**
- Tee at Rs 999 is double Veirdo and Rs 200-460 over Souled Store for "100% cotton" both
  claim. The only reason to pay is the GSM, and that's a number I can't feel through a phone.
- Joggers at Rs 1,499 equals Van Heusen's top price with zero reviews. I'd want a launch price
  or a combo (tee + joggers) before I try.

**H. Trust**
- Brand looks designed, not established. No "Made in India", no maker story, no real people.
- Without reviews: no, at these prices.
- One thing that makes me distrust it: finding out the photos are AI and the product looks
  different. One review saying "doesn't look like the photos" ends it.

**I. One-star review I'd write**
> **Pics are fake, sizes are tiny**
> The photos are clearly AI and the real pants don't fall like that. Ordered L, it's tight,
> there's no XL. Called joggers, they're wide-leg pyjamas. Rs 1,499 for this?

**J. Five-star review**
No five-star review yet: nothing on the listing proves the fabric, and that's the only reason
to pay this much.

## 5. Top 10 fixes I'd demand

1. Replace most AI model shots with real photos of the real garments on real people (SFY
   already has workshop footage and blue-tee/black-jogger photos in `assets/sfy/`).
2. Add XL and XXL at minimum (every competitor goes to 2XL-5XL), or say clearly "S-L only".
3. Rename the joggers to what they are: wide-leg / baggy track pants. Baggy is selling (men's
   track pant bestsellers #5, #6, #19); "joggers" mismatches the photo.
4. Back "100% cotton" and "230/320 GSM" with a supplier spec sheet or lab report, and show
   the care label in a photo.
5. Say "pre-shrunk" (only if true) and add a wash-care panel; shrinking and fading are the
   top complaints on every competitor.
6. Use S/M/L/XL on the charts to match Amazon's size picker; drop FS/FM/MS codes.
7. Remove "UNISEX" from fitted-tee size guides; keep separate men's and women's charts.
8. Add model height and size worn to every model image.
9. Add jogger inseam, thigh and rise; and a fabric-weight proof (fabric next to a regular tee).
10. Cut page-5 text to 5-6 readable labels; make the icon row large enough to read at phone size.

## 6. What would make me buy today

1. Real photos.
2. XL in stock.
3. A GSM/composition proof image.
4. "Pre-shrunk" with care instructions.
5. A launch price or tee + jogger combo, until reviews exist.
